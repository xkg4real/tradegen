### Dimension 2 — Statistical Correctness of the Odds Engine

6. **`StationaryBootstrap` demeaning (`§7.2`) states two mutually contradictory formulas, both of which violate market bounds, and omits `high` needed by `BetLeg`.**
   - **Evidence**:
     - [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:428-431`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r1/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L428-L431) first says simple returns *"are then demeaned so that E[1 + R] = 1"* (additive shift $R_t - \bar{R}$) and immediately says *"Each day's open, low and close, all relative to the previous close, are multiplied by the same factor $1 / \text{mean}(1 + R_{\text{close}})$"* (multiplicative scaling $(1 + R_x)/\mu$).
     - Both formulas break path mechanics:
       1. If gross returns $(1+R_{\text{open}}, 1+R_{\text{low}}, 1+R_{\text{close}})$ are divided by $\mu = \text{mean}(1 + R_{\text{close}})$: when $\mu < 1$ (any asset with negative sample drift), dividing by $\mu < 1$ scales gross returns **up**, pushing KRX days above $+30\%$ ($1 + \tilde{R}_{\text{close}} > 1.30$) and breaking `structural_max(plan)` = $1.3^n$ (`L512-513`). Conversely, when $\mu > 1$, dividing $1 + R_{\text{open}} = 1.0$ by $\mu > 1$ turns every flat open into an artificial overnight gap-down ($1/\mu < 1.0$), triggering false gap-through stop fills at `min(open, stop)` (`L492-493`)!
       2. If additive demeaning $R_t - \bar{R}$ is used on volatile crypto when $\bar{R} > 0$, $1 + R_{\text{low}} - \bar{R}$ can become $\le 0$ (negative prices) or breach $-30\%$ on KRX.
     - Furthermore, [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:426, 430`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r1/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L426-L430) resamples only `(open, low, close)`—omitting `high`—even though `BetLeg` (`L434, 437-438`) is a `+u / -d` bracket whose upper target `+u` requires `high` to detect intraday first-passage times!
   - **Concrete fix**: (a) Resample full `(open, high, low, close)` bars; (b) remove drift by tilting the stationary bootstrap's single-day selection probabilities $q_t > 0$ ($\sum_t q_t = 1$, minimum Kullback-Leibler divergence / exponential tilting from uniform $1/N$ such that $\sum_t q_t (1 + R_{\text{close}, t}) = 1$), or adjust only the close-to-open / open-to-close return with explicit clamping to market limits while preserving intraday ratios `(high/open, low/open, close/open)` untouched; and (c) specify the tie-breaking rule when both `high >= entry*(1+u)` and `low <= entry*(1-d)` occur on the same daily bar (fail-closed: stop `-d` triggers first unless open already gapped above `+u`).

7. **Multi-asset FX product returns and KRX limit-down carry violate $E[1 + R_{\text{KRW}}] = 1$ and falsely trip the `HIDDEN_EDGE` bug alarm.**
   - **Evidence**:
     - [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:433, 454-455, 558-559`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r1/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L433) includes `KRW=X` as a jointly resampled row for US legs and converts Binance USDT spot to KRW via Upbit `KRW-USDT` for OKX legs, while `HIDDEN_EDGE` (`L558-559`) fires a red bug banner if the simulated mean of `(ending equity + costs paid + house edge accrued)` differs from `W0` by more than 4 standard errors.
     - Under joint resampling of an asset return $R_{\text{asset}}$ and FX return $R_{\text{FX}}$ that were demeaned separately ($E[1+R_{\text{asset}}]=1$, $E[1+R_{\text{FX}}]=1$), the KRW gross return is $(1+R_{\text{asset}})(1+R_{\text{FX}})$, whose expectation is $1 + \text{Cov}(R_{\text{asset}}, R_{\text{FX}}) \ne 1$. At $n = 1,000,000$ paths (`L498`), 4 standard errors over 30 days is $\sim 4 \cdot \sigma_{30\text{d}} / 1000 \approx 0.04\%$, so any historical asset-FX covariance above $\sim 1.5\text{ bp/day}$ systematically trips `HIDDEN_EDGE`!
     - Similarly, on KRX paths with limit-down lock (`L432, 494-496`), a position that attempts to exit on a `limit_down` day is forced to hold into day $t+1$. In `StationaryBootstrap` (with geometric block continuation probability $1 - 1/20 = 0.95$), day $t+1$ is 95% of the time the actual historical day following a limit-down day, whose conditional historical mean return $E[1 + R_{t+1} \mid \text{limit\_down}_t]$ is not 1, again breaking the martingale identity and tripping `HIDDEN_EDGE`.
   - **Concrete fix**: Express every leg's OHLC series in **KRW before demeaning and before fitting `GarchT`** (so $E[1 + R_{\text{KRW}}] = 1$ holds directly on the KRW return series, while FX conversion spread `fx_spread_bps` remains a trading cost), and either demean post-limit-down continuation returns separately or count the conditional post-limit-down drift term explicitly in the `HIDDEN_EDGE` accounting identity.

8. **The optional-stopping ceiling $(W_0 - L)/(T - L)$ is invalid whenever a step can gap below $L$, directly contradicting Acceptance Test 2 (`L640`).**
   - **Evidence**:
     - [`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:61-62, 509-511, 560-562, 640`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r1/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L509-L511) uses $(W_0 - L)/(T - L)$ as the optional-stopping ceiling and requires in §8 Test 2 (`L640`): *"Ceiling: randomised no-edge plans never exceed $(W_0 - L)/(T - L)$ beyond the 99.9% lower bound."*
     - By Doob's optional stopping theorem for a non-negative supermartingale $W_t \ge 0$ stopped at $\tau = \inf\{t : W_t \ge T \text{ or } W_t \le L\}$:
       $$W_0 \ge E[W_\tau] \ge T \cdot P(W_\tau \ge T) + E[W_\tau \mid W_\tau \le L]\bigl(1 - P(W_\tau \ge T)\bigr) \implies P(W_\tau \ge T) \le \frac{W_0 - E[W_\tau \mid W_\tau \le L]}{T - E[W_\tau \mid W_\tau \le L]}.$$
       When a trade with stop $-d$ is sized up to $\text{stake} = (W - L)/d > W - L$ (`L413`) and a daily bar gaps through the stop (`L492-493`: fills at `min(open, stop)`) or suffers KRX limit-down carry (`L494-496`), stopping equity $W_\tau$ overshoots below $L$ ($E[W_\tau \mid W_\tau \le L] < L$). Because $x \mapsto (W_0 - x)/(T - x)$ is strictly decreasing, $P(W_\tau \ge T)$ can legitimately exceed $(W_0 - L)/(T - L)$ (up to $W_0 / T$) on a true zero-edge martingale!
     - Although `L509-510` adds a `gap_possible` branch returning $W_0 / T$, under `GarchT` (`Z_BOUND = 22` in [`core/quant/garch.py:594`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r1/core/quant/garch.py#L594)) and `StationaryBootstrap` (any historical low or open beyond $-d$), `gap_possible` is **always True** whenever $\text{stake} > W - L$, making $(W_0 - L)/(T - L)$ inapplicable whenever a stop $-d < 1$ is used to leverage the risk budget, and causing Test 2 (`L640`) to fail.
   - **Concrete fix**: Define `gap_possible = (stake_max_loss_at_worst_bound > W - L)` (which is `False` only when $\text{stake} \le W - L$ on a $1\times$ long spot position without limit-down carry cost exceeding $L$, or when stop fills are guaranteed without gap overshoot), and update §8 Test 2 (`L640`) and §7.5 (`L560-562`) to test against `ceiling(W0, L, T, gap_possible)` (or $(W_0 - L_{\min})/(T - L_{\min})$ where $L_{\min}$ is the worst-case post-gap equity floor).

    - **Evidence**:
