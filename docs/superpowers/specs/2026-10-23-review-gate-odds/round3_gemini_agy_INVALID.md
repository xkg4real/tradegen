### 3. `TwoPointBootstrap` intraday KRX band clamp makes limit-up/limit-down opens absorbing states ($P(\text{unlock at close}) = 0$), omits KRX tick rounding of $[D, U]$, and leaves multi-step limit-down carry ambiguous

- **Evidence**:
  - Spec [`2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:624-634`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L624-L634).
  - Codebase [`core/engine/orders.py:414-431`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L414-L431) and [`L492-L509`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L492-L509).
- **Problem**:
  1. **Intraday absorption at $D$ and $U$**: In [`spec:625-630`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L625-L630), a KRX session has a single band $[D, U]$ around the previous session's close $p_{\text{ref}}$. On the overnight step (`prev_close -> open`), $p = p_{\text{ref}} \in (D, U)$. If the overnight draw lands at the lower band $p_{\text{open}} = D$ (limit-down open), then on the subsequent intraday step (`open -> close`) starting from $p = p_{\text{open}} = D$, the spec computes:
     $$x_u = \min(D \cdot e^m, U) > D, \qquad x_d = \max(D \cdot e^{-m}, D) = D$$
     $$q = \frac{p - x_d}{x_u - x_d} = \frac{D - D}{x_u - D} = 0$$
     Thus $p_{\text{close}} = x_d = D$ with probability $1 - q = 1$ for **every** $m > 0$! Symmetrically, if $p_{\text{open}} = U$ (limit-up open), $x_u = \min(U \cdot e^m, U) = U$, so $q = (U - x_d)/(U - x_d) = 1$ and $p_{\text{close}} = U$ with probability $1$. Any KRX path that hits $D$ or $U$ at the open is mathematically locked at $D$ or $U$ for the rest of the session and can never bounce intraday.
  2. **Missing tick rounding on $D, U$**: [`core/engine/orders.py:414-419`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L414-L419) (`limit_prices(prev_close, price_limit, tick)`) floors $U$ and ceils $D$ to the KRX price-band tick (`krx_tick`), whereas [`spec:625`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L625) uses raw `reference price × (1 ∓ dated price_limit)`.
  3. **Carried limit-down exit check point & consecutive locks**: [`spec:633`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L633) says a check at $D$ that would exit carries to the next session at $2\times$ cost (`orders.LIMIT_DOWN_CARRY_COST_MULTIPLIER`), but does not state whether a lock at the open check carries to the **same session's close** (if unlocked) or to the **next session's open**, nor whether consecutive limit-down locks keep the flat $2\times$ multiplier of [`orders.py:505`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L505) or compound.
- **Concrete fix**:
  1. Compute $[D, U]$ via `orders.limit_prices(prev_close, orders.krx_price_limit(market, session_date), orders.krx_tick(prev_close, market))` (or keep unrounded only when `tick=0`).
  2. To preserve the conditional martingale property $\mathbb{E}[p_{\text{close}} \mid p_{\text{open}}] = p_{\text{open}}$ **and** keep $p_{\text{close}} \in [D, U]$ without boundary absorption at the open, clamp the overnight step to the interior band $[D_{\text{open}}, U_{\text{open}}] = [(1-\epsilon)D + \epsilon p_{\text{ref}}, (1-\epsilon)U + \epsilon p_{\text{ref}}]$ (or draw the session close directly from $p_{\text{ref}}$ when $p_{\text{open}} \in \{D, U\}$, or only clamp at the session-level close while checking open against $(D, U)$). Actually, the cleanest martingale-preserving rule is: no trade can execute when locked at $D$ or $U$; if $p_{\text{open}}$ is clamped only at the session level by drawing the full-session close conditional on $p_{\text{ref}}$ whenever $p_{\text{open}}$ hits $\{D, U\}$? Wait—if $\mathbb{E}[p_{\text{close}} \mid p_{\text{open}} = D] = D$ and $p_{\text{close}} \ge D$ almost surely, then by basic probability $p_{\text{close}} = D$ almost surely (`E[X - D] = 0` with `X - D >= 0 => X = D a.s.`)! That is a fundamental theorem of martingales: **any** martingale bounded below by $D$ that reaches $D$ at the open **must** stay at $D$ at the close! Therefore, if KRX daily price limits $[D, U]$ apply to the entire session relative to `prev_close`, an open at $D$ cannot both be a martingale from open to close and stay $\ge D$ unless the open step is strictly inside $(D, U)$ (or the daily band is enforced on the two-step composition where only the close check can lock at $D$ / $U$, matching [`core/engine/orders.py:423-431`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L423-L431) which only locks at session `close`). Specify that the KRX price-limit band $[D, U]$ clamps the **close** (and open is clamped to $[D + \text{tick}, U - \text{tick}]$ so $p_{\text{open}} \in (D, U)$ strictly), and that a limit-down lock at close $t$ carries the pending exit to the first unlocked check point of session $t+1$ (open, then close) with a flat $2\times$ variable exit cost (`2 * \kappa_s`, matching [`orders.py:505`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/core/engine/orders.py#L505)).

### 4. Bold-sizing (`bold()`) fails when $T - W$ is more than one win away or when lot-rounded down, and `REACHED` stopping condition 1 contradicts `reached_by_deadline` on compounding paths

- **Evidence**:
  - Spec [`2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:592-597`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L592-L597) and [`L699-L705`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L699-L705).
- **Problem**:
  1. **Bold play when $s_{\text{affordable}} < s_{\text{reach}}$**: [`spec:592-595`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L592-L595) defines `bold()` as: *"the largest $s$ that satisfies affordability and does not overshoot $T$ on a win: $s(u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1 + u)) - \phi_b - \phi_s \ge T - W$ is the reaching condition, and $s$ is reduced until it just holds."* Whenever $T - W$ is larger than a single win can achieve (e.g., step 1 of Worked Example 1 where $W = 42,000$, $T - W = 41,958,000$), **no** affordable stake satisfies the reaching inequality. Dubins–Savage bold play is $s = \min(s_{\text{affordable}}, s_{\text{reach}})$: bet the maximum affordable stake when far from $T$, and cap at $s_{\text{reach}}$ when one win can reach $T$.
  2. **Lot rounding down misses $T$**: When $s_{\text{reach}} \le s_{\text{affordable}}$, [`spec:593-594`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L593-L594) reduces $s$ until equality holds ($s_{\text{reach}} = \frac{T - W + \phi_b + \phi_s}{u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1+u)}$), and [`spec:597`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L597) then rounds stakes **down** to whole shares (`KRX_STOCK`, `KRX_ETF`) or whole contracts. Rounding $s_{\text{reach}}$ down by even a fraction of a share strictly violates the reaching condition ($W_{\text{after win}} < T$), so a win of $+u$ fails to trigger `REACHED`!
  3. **Stopping condition 1 (`REACHED`) vs `reached_by_deadline`**: [`spec:699-700`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L699-L700) states that **every** simulated path stops at the first check point where liquidation value $\ge T$ (`REACHED`). Yet [`spec:704-705`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L704-L705) states that for compounding paths, `reached_by_deadline` (liquidation value $\ge T$ at some check point) *"can be true while the terminal outcome is FLOOR or RUNNING."* If every path stops at the first check point where liquidation value $\ge T$, it is impossible for a path to hit $\ge T$ and later end in `FLOOR` or `RUNNING`.
- **Concrete fix**:
  1. Define `bold()` explicitly as:
     $$s_{\text{reach}} = \text{lot\_ceil}\!\left(\frac{\max(0,\, T - W + \phi_b + \phi_s)}{u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1 + u)}\right), \qquad s_{\text{aff}} = \text{lot\_floor}(s_{\max\text{\_affordable}}), \qquad s_{\text{bold}} = \min(s_{\text{aff}},\, s_{\text{reach}})$$
     refusing when $u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1+u) \le 0$ or $s_{\text{aff}} < \text{min\_order\_size}$.
  2. Add a boolean `stop_at_target: bool = True` to `GoalPlan`: target-stopping plans (`stop_at_target=True`) stop at the first check point where liquidation value $\ge T$ (`REACHED == reached_by_deadline`), whereas horizon-compounding plans (`stop_at_target=False`, e.g. card strategies evaluated over a fixed horizon) only stop early on `FLOOR`, evaluate `REACHED` vs `FLOOR` vs `RUNNING` at `DEADLINE`, and record `reached_by_deadline = any(W_t >= T for t <= H)` along the path.

### 4. Bold-sizing (`bold()`) fails when $T - W$ is more than one win away or when lot-rounded down, and `REACHED` stopping condition 1 contradicts `reached_by_deadline` on compounding paths

  - Spec [`2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md:592-597`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L592-L597) and [`L699-L705`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L699-L705).

  2. **Lot rounding down misses $T$**: When $s_{\text{reach}} \le s_{\text{affordable}}$, [`spec:593-594`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L593-L594) reduces $s$ until equality holds ($s_{\text{reach}} = \frac{T - W + \phi_b + \phi_s}{u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1+u)}$), and [`spec:597`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L597) then rounds stakes **down** to whole shares (`KRX_STOCK`, `KRX_ETF`) or whole contracts. Rounding $s_{\text{reach}}$ down by even a fraction of a share strictly violates the reaching condition ($W_{\text{after win}} < T$), so a win of $+u$ fails to trigger `REACHED`!
  3. **Stopping condition 1 (`REACHED`) vs `reached_by_deadline`**: [`spec:699-700`](file:///C:/Users/whydi/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-whydi-OneDrive-Desktop-TRADEGEN/b1492ead-23f3-4704-b198-5b5fc445ad00/scratchpad/tg_review_r3_gem/docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-23-compliance-gate-and-odds-engine-design.md#L699-L700) states that **every** simulated path stops at the first check point where liquidation value $\ge T$ (`REACHED`). - **Concrete fix**:
  1. Define `bold()` explicitly as:
     $$s_{\text{reach}} = \text{lot\_ceil}\!\left(\frac{\max(0,\, T - W + \phi_b + \phi_s)}{u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1 + u)}\right), \qquad s_{\text{aff}} = \text{lot\_floor}(s_{\max\text{\_affordable}}), \qquad s_{\text{bold}} = \min(s_{\text{aff}},\, s_{\text{reach}})$$
     refusing when $u - \kappa_b - \kappa_s(1+u) \le 0$ or $s_{\text{aff}} < \text{min\_order\_size}$.
  2. Add `stop_at_target: bool = True` to `GoalPlan`: target-stopping plans (`stop_at_target=True`) stop at the first check point where liquidation value $\ge T$ (`REACHED == reached_by_deadline`), whereas horizon-compounding plans (`stop_at_target=False`) only stop early on `FLOOR`, classify `REACHED` vs `FLOOR` vs `RUNNING` at `DEADLINE`, and record `reached_by_deadline = any(W_t >= T for t <= H)` along the path.
