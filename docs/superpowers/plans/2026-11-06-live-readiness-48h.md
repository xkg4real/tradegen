## Critical path and limits

Capture-bound code must be reviewed and frozen before a newly registered study
starts. An actual 48-hour study starting after this development begins cannot
also finish within the same 48-hour wall-clock target unless the observation
protocol itself is separately redesigned and justified. The requested deadline
does not provide missing data, make a strategy pass, or waive owner signatures.

The currently available Upbit snapshots do not certify every intra-interval
price or account event. Implementing a generic verified evidence contract does
not supply a production attestor. An unavailable attestor remains an explicit
launch blocker, even if all mocked contract tests pass.

At equity equal to the protected peak, equality alone is not a risk breach.
There can nevertheless be zero available cost/loss budget. Positive fees or
spread may move marked equity below the unchanged floor; the current sizing
can return `STUCK_AT_START`. Neither an extra deposit with a reset peak nor a
different valuation label is an authorized solution. A first-order date cannot
be inferred solely from coding completion.

KIS Live market viewing is distinct from KIS private account evidence, execution
and validated strategy selection. Broader venue coverage needs its own complete
economic, FX, calendar and source contracts; no fallback to a different market
silently bypasses the user's unchanged safeguards.
