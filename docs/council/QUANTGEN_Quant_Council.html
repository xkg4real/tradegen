What the quants overturned

The Korea volatility target had been judged on an eleven-year file; on 1997–2026 it is an indicative pass at ETF cost. The 2 Oct "close" was a mid-session print from a file written at 10:56 KST (official 7,003.74). The staleness rule and the drawdown ladder would have frozen a literal build.

Where the quants were wrong

QF's trial-count formula was not the cited paper's. Corrected, momentum vol-scaling is a borderline pass and the Daniel–Moskowitz gate fails. The jump-model regime engine was tuned on part of its own hold-out, so it is not built.

What it means for the owner

The Korea card stays BUILD-AS-INSURANCE on a formal pass. With every compared variant counted (selection factor 1.26–2.87 against a 1.282 threshold), it is expected to return INSUFFICIENT DATA. Six owner decisions carry stated defaults.

Step 1 · Quantitative Finance panel

Seven quants filed 180 audit items, and most of them hit the measuring tools

Each member took one lane, re-opened the sources the documents cite, recomputed their numbers on local data and piloted proposals at QUANTGEN's own costs. A false true is something the documents accepted that is wrong; a false error is something they rejected that is right.

Step 2 · three-way cross-examination

The other panels upheld most findings and broke two

The Professors and the Executives ruled on every QF item and re-ran QF's scripts. Two independent data labs re-implemented the contested tests from the spec's own definitions. The disagreements turned out to be about definitions, not arithmetic.

What the data labs settled

  • DL-A, protective cards. Its harness reproduced all 385 published golden quantities, and every QF Korea and US number under its author's definitions. The +12.7 vs +3.8 pp Korea gap came from a start date after the June-1997 peak.
  • DL-B, estimators and regimes. 28 tests: 19 replicated, 6 partly replicated, 3 corrected, none failed. SMA20 sits outside the 90% model confidence set under QLIKE, but not under MSE.
  • QF's own errors. Its independent-trial formula was the Kish form, not the paper's; with an honest trial count, R-03 passes at 0.98 and R-04 fails at 0.77. The jump-model penalty was set on markets inside its own hold-out.
  • Concessions. The Professors withdrew their ban on HAR models built on daily ranges (competitive at every horizon once the range is modelled), while the ML ban stands; the Executives accepted the USD/KRW leg's REJECT; QF withdrew its "venue artefact" framing of the US card.

Step 3 · council of three chairs

Twelve resolutions settle the gate once, blind, before any card runs

Each chair ruled on every contested or qualified item and every proposal. Unanimous rulings were adopted, two-to-one rulings carried the third chair's compatible conditions, and red lines bound the wording.

Red lines that bound the wording

  • Professors. No promotion on one path or one crisis. No gate rule chosen after seeing the card it decides. A forward record never validates.
  • Executives. No regime-model state, acting or displayed. No decisions on provisional or stale inputs. Risk-model inputs never halt the book. No instrument the desk can't trade at the declared cost and size.
  • Quantitative Finance. No REJECT on single-path drawdown. Verdicts are book-specific. Nothing may freeze the book.

Step 4 · adversarial spec review

Four reviewers attacked spec v2.0 until each of them agreed

The adversarial-spec protocol was run with four independent reviewer personas: a QA engineer, a data engineer, a junior developer standing in for Claude Code, and a risk officer combined with an on-call engineer. Claude critiqued alongside them and ruled on every conflict.

What the review changed

  • HALT is per book. Only an acting input can halt its own book. Risk-model, context and forward-sim failures fall back or are logged.
  • Schemas are the contract. Every rule output has a field, and every example in the spec validates.
  • Clocks are effective-dated. KRX close bounds are 06:00:30Z from June 2015 to July 2016 and 06:30:30Z after, with exchange-specific bounds elsewhere.
  • The selection factor is computed, not declared. Every distinct configuration counts, the data cut-off is pinned to the last official closes (KR 2 Oct, US 1 Oct 2026), and the larger factor binds.
  • Governance cannot be gamed quietly. Verdicts carry the package hash, forward records are append-only, an operator kill counts against the record, and a redesign is allowed only once.

Competitive brief

Against public institutional practice, v2.0 closes most gaps in measurement

33 capabilities, each scored 0–3 against what published sources describe: papers, regulators, vendor methodologies and public firm research, never any firm's internal practice. The scores are the council report's reading, not a council vote.

Where QUANTGEN stands

  • Strengths. Clean timing discipline, conservative costs, and a desk that publishes its refusals as well as its passes.
  • Weaknesses. No own fills to calibrate costs, a single data vendor, and an acting estimator the challenger beats on forecasts.
  • Opportunities. Logging is cheap: vintages, first prints, implied-vol snapshots and leverage context cost little to add now.
  • Threats. The Korea card and the gate package are fragile. A single crash month can swing a verdict.
