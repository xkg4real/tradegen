## KQ1. Cost-of-carry fair value and the KOSPI200 futures basis (funding rate, dividends, empirical basis, roll, program flows)

### Takeaway
F = S·exp((r − q)T) is the textbook forward (Shreve II Thm 5.6.2, p. 241) and equals the futures price only when rates are deterministic (5.6.11, p. 247); for KOSPI200 the operational fair value published by KRX/brokers uses a 91-day CD rate and a dividend-amount index, dividends are concentrated at the December record date, and the empirical literature finds persistent underpricing in Korea tied to short-sale restrictions and a measurable ex-dividend basis break — so the app should treat "basis vs fair" as a monitor with a cost band, not a naive arbitrage.

Shreve-Stochastic Calculus for Finance II_20150303200346.pdf`, pdf page = printed + 16).

### Inferences
- Funding rate choice: use the 91-day CD rate (매일 published; FRED does not carry it — source is KOFIA/BOK ECOS, UNVERIFIED as keyless) to reproduce KIS/KRX `hts_thpr`, and report a second fair value on KOFR (compounded overnight, the RFR the club's SOFR-era conventions map to). The difference CD − KOFR is the funding basis and should be shown in bp.
- Dividend treatment: a continuous q is wrong for Korea; model a discrete dividend-point index with essentially all points falling at the December record date (the reform is reducing this share). A March contract quoted in December therefore carries a large negative "fair basis"; the Yuanta and Ko numbers show the realised ex-dividend basis break is smaller than theory (market opens 1.07 pts above the theoretical drop; basis contracts 0.85% vs 1.18%).
- Roll/calendar spread: fair spread = F_far − F_near = S·[e^{(r_far)T_far} − e^{(r_near)T_near}] − (dividend points between the two expiries)·e^{r(…)}; the Mirae note's two corrections (call rate vs CD; margin financing) are the practical adjustments. Empirically the roll is dominated by the December dividend assumption.
- A retail account cannot execute cash-and-carry against the 200-stock basket; the executable expressions are (i) a basis/mispricing monitor with a cost band (Gay & Jung), (ii) roll timing on the calendar spread, and (iii) using 차익 program-trade net flow as a coincident indicator of arbitrageur activity.

## KQ2. Implied volatility: Black-76 inversion of the KIS board, SVI, SABR, no-arbitrage checks, model-free variance / VKOSPI, Breeden–Litzenberger density

### Takeaway
KIS's `display_board_callput` already returns, per expiry, up to 100 calls and 100 puts with strike, bid/ask/last, open interest, Greeks (delta, gamma, vega, theta, rho), HTS implied vol and HTS theoretical price — so the app can (a) re-invert prices with its own Black-76 on the futures price and reconcile against `hts_ints_vltl`, (b) fit SVI slices with the Gatheral–Jacquier static-arbitrage conditions (the existing SABR fitter is a second parameterisation, not a replacement), (c) compute the Cboe/KRX fair-variance-swap strip and reconcile against VKOSPI/VIX, and (d) differentiate the fitted call price twice for the risk-neutral density.

## KQ5. The hedger's P&L identity and the daily-frequency simulation (GARCH-t P-paths + Black-76 Q-pricer)

### Takeaway
The user's paper states the identity exactly — the replication cost minus e^{rT}V_0(σ_h) equals ½∫₀ᵀ e^{r(T−t)} Γ_t S_t² (σ_t² − σ_h²) dt (eq. 34, §6.7, p. 16, attributed to El Karoui, Jeanblanc-Picqué & Shreve) — and Shreve II supplies the Greeks (pp. 159–160) and the "all these effects exactly cancel" statement for a GBM hedged at the true σ (p. 162); the app can simulate it path-by-path with daily rebalancing and test the identity as a regression with slope one.

### Cited Findings
- From_Radon-Nikodym_to_Fung-Hsieh §6.7 p. 16: "Delta-hedging a European claim with Black–Scholes deltas computed at a volatility σ_h, when the realised volatility is σ_t, produces a terminal replication error that El Karoui, Jeanblanc-Picqué and Shreve [20] showed to be: cost of replication − e^{rT}V_0(σ_h) = ½ ∫₀ᵀ e^{r(T−t)} Γ_t S_t² (σ_t² − σ_h²) dt (34), with Γ_t the gamma of the claim. The right-hand side is the familiar hedger's P&L in [E5] and §3 of the OTM notes, with the sign of a long synthetic position … if σ_h happens to equal the realised volatility, the cost is exactly e^{rT}V_0(σ_real). The option buyer pays e^{rT}V_0(σ_Q); the replicator pays, in expectation, roughly e^{rT}V_0(σ_P)." Prop. 1 (35): E^Q[e^{−rT}Π_T] = 0 and E^P[Π_T] = −Cov^P(Z_T, Π_T); "Replication does not earn the volatility premium; it avoids paying it, and that is all." ### Inferences
- Simulation design (daily, N = trading days to expiry, Δ = 1/252): for each P-path S_0…S_N from the GARCH-t engine (drift mode "premium" or "zero"; conditional variance path σ_t² is known per path), price the claim at t = 0 with Black-76 at σ_h (from the KIS board IV or the SVI fit, using F_t = S_t e^{r(T−t)} or the actual futures), hold δ_t = Black-76 delta at σ_h, rebalance at each close, finance at r (CD91); Π_T = payoff − Σ hedge gains − e^{rT}V_0(σ_h). Compute the identity's right-hand side with the discrete sum ½ Σ_i e^{r(T−t_i)} Γ_i S_i² (r_i²/Δ − σ_h²) Δ where r_i² /Δ is the path's realised daily variance (or the model's σ_i² for the "expected" version).
- Validation criteria: (i) on GBM paths with σ_h = σ_true, mean Π_T → 0 and its std shrinks with 1/√N (discretisation only); (ii) on GBM with σ_h ≠ σ_true, regress Π_T on the discrete identity: slope in [0.9, 1.1], R² > 0.9; (iii) on GARCH-t paths, sign of mean Π_T equals sign of (E[σ_t²] − σ_h²) and, in rf mode, mean of e^{−rT}Π_T is within one 5,000-path standard error of zero (Prop. 1); (iv) the term-by-term decomposition (gamma cost vs financing) is displayed in bp of notional for the structuring page.
- What the structuring page gets: for a reverse convertible / PPN the dealer's hedge P&L distribution under P with the traded σ_h, the break-even realised vol (σ_real at which Π_T = 0), and the tail (CVaR of Π_T) — all in bp of notional.

## Candidates for QUANTGEN

Conventions: rates in percent, changes in bp, option premium in bp of notional (notional = index × 250,000 KRW per contract for KOSPI200; ES = index × US$50); all backtests signal on close t and earn from t+1 with explicit costs (`core/quant/backtest.py`); every model reports its own validation number and the guardrail flags in the narrative.

### 1. Black-76 board inversion, parity and static-arbitrage checks (model)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 index options (monthly, Thursday/Monday weeklies, mini); SPX/ES options via yfinance/KIS overseas.
- **Family**: structural (pricing model).
- **Mechanism**: for each row of `display_board_callput`, mid = (bid+ask)/2, F = same-expiry futures price, df = e^{−r_CD T}; solve Black-76(F,K,T,σ,df) = mid by Brent (`_invert`, `swaps.py:256`) for OTM series; parity residual ρ_K = C − P − df(F − K); vertical/butterfly/calendar inequalities from KQ2.
- **Sources**: Shreve II §4.5.4–4.5.6 pp. 158–162 (BSM function, Greeks, forward value 4.5.26); Cboe mathematics methodology (F and K_0 rule); KIS chk_display_board_callput column list.
- **User-material refs**: Shreve II pp. 159–162; paper formula sheet p. 19 (Breeden–Litzenberger, spanning).
- **Data needs**: daily board snapshot per expiry (KIS), same-expiry futures (KIS), CD91 (KOFIA/BOK), KRX calendar; min history: none for the model, 60 daily snapshots for the drift-of-IV statistics.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt (`display_board_callput`, `display_board_futures`, `inquire_price`); yfinance (SPX chain) for the U.S. leg.
- **Backtest design**: n/a (model); daily reconciliation job.
- **Pre-registered validation**: (a) median |σ_app − hts_ints_vltl| ≤ 0.5 vol pt over series with bid > 0 and premium ≥ 0.05 pt, and 90th percentile ≤ 1.5 vol pt; (b) parity residual |ρ_K| ≤ half the summed bid-ask spread on ≥ 95% of strikes with |K − F| ≤ 10% F; (c) zero butterfly violations after mid-price smoothing; any failure is displayed, not silently corrected.
- **Expected edge / decay**: none (a measurement); parity violations beyond costs are expected to be rare post-2017 (foreign-institution-dominated market, Sol Kim 2026).
- **Complexity**: 2. **Caveats**: KIS IV definition unknown (spot vs futures underlying); stale quotes at 15:45; weeklies' expiry code format UNVERIFIED.

### 2. SVI slice fit with Gatheral–Jacquier no-arbitrage (model), SABR as cross-check
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 options; SPX.
- **Family**: structural.
- **Mechanism**: per expiry, fit total variance w(k) = a + b[ρ(k − m) + √((k − m)² + σ²)], k = ln(K/F), by least squares on mid IVs weighted by 1/spread (vega-weighted); enforce b ≥ 0, |ρ| < 1, a + bσ√(1 − ρ²) ≥ 0 and check the Gatheral–Jacquier butterfly function g(k) ≥ 0; across expiries check ∂w/∂T ≥ 0 at fixed k; run `calibrate_sabr` (β = 1) on the same slice and report both RMSEs.
- **Sources**: Gatheral & Jacquier 2014 QF 14(1) 59–71 (arXiv 1204.0646); Hagan et al. 2002 Wilmott pp. 84–108; Gatheral 2004 (SVI origin, snippet-level).
- **User-material refs**: paper §3.5 (smile as the shape of Z, p. 9–10 per TOC) and formula sheet p. 19; Shreve II p. 162 (vega).
- **Data needs**: candidate 1's IV table; ≥ 8 OTM strikes per expiry; min 1 day.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt; yfinance.
- **Backtest design**: n/a; daily fit log.
- **Pre-registered validation**: RMSE ≤ 0.5 vol pt within |k| ≤ 0.15 and ≤ 1.0 vol pt within |k| ≤ 0.30 on the front two monthly expiries; g(k) ≥ 0 on the fitted range; no calendar crossing between consecutive expiries; SABR RMSE reported alongside (expected worse in the wings); parameter stability: day-over-day change in ρ has |Δρ| ≤ 0.2 on 95% of days.
- **Expected edge / decay**: none; feeds candidates 3, 4, 8, 10.
- **Complexity**: 3. **Caveats**: KOSPI200 weeklies have 17 strikes only; far OTM puts with bid 0 must be excluded, which biases the wing.

### 3. Model-free implied variance and VKOSPI / VIX replication; Breeden–Litzenberger density (model)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 options; SPX.
- **Family**: structural.
- **Mechanism**: Cboe strip σ_T² = (2/T)Σ(ΔK_i/K_i²)e^{RT}Q(K_i) − (1/T)(F/K_0 − 1)² on near/next expiries, two-consecutive-zero-bid truncation, 30-day interpolation in minutes (formula in KQ2); density f_Q(K) = e^{rT}∂²C/∂K² from the SVI call price (analytic in k); pricing kernel proxy Z = f_Q/f_P with f_P from the Quant Lab's GARCH-t terminal distribution at the same horizon.
- **Sources**: Cboe Volatility Index Mathematics Methodology pp. 7–9; Britten-Jones & Neuberger 2000; Jiang & Tian 2005 RFS 18(4); Breeden & Litzenberger 1978 J. Business 51(4); KB/KRX VKOSPI description (30-day, fair variance swap, ATM/OTM, launched 2009-04-13).
- **User-material refs**: paper §5.1 p. 11 eqs. (22)–(24) and the VIX discretisation; formula sheet p. 19; Meucci P vs Q §3.1 (risk premium as the bridge; per ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW).
- **Data needs**: daily board for near/next expiries; VKOSPI daily close (KRX 정보데이터시스템; keyless access UNVERIFIED); `^VIX` (yfinance) for the U.S. leg.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt; yfinance; other: KRX data portal.
- **Backtest design**: n/a.
- **Pre-registered validation**: |MFIV_app − VKOSPI| ≤ 0.5 vol pt on ≥ 90% of days and ≤ 1.0 on ≥ 99% (KRX's formula differs in detail, so a persistent bias up to 0.3 pt is acceptable if stable); U.S.: |σ_app − VIX close| ≤ 0.3 vol pt on ≥ 90% of days using yfinance SPX mids; density: f_Q ≥ 0 everywhere and ∫f_Q dK ∈ [0.99, 1.01]; E^Q[S_T] within 0.2% of F.
- **Expected edge / decay**: none directly; MFIV is the input to candidates 5 and 6 and the app's "implied vs GARCH" tile.
- **Complexity**: 3. **Caveats**: KRX VKOSPI exact formula unread; ΔK edge treatment at the 2.5-pt grid; near-month within 7 days must roll.

### 4. Futures fair value, basis monitor and calendar-spread roll (arbitrage / carry monitor)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 futures (front, next), mini KOSPI200, KOSDAQ150; ES/NQ.
- **Family**: arbitrage (monitor), carry (roll).
- **Mechanism**: F* = (S − D_pts)e^{r T} with D_pts the dividend points expected before expiry (December-concentrated; Ko 2022; Yuanta 2022) and r = CD91 (KRX convention per the 2003 Mirae note) and, separately, r = KOFR-compounded; mispricing m_t = (F − F*)/S in bp; cost band ±c from commission + spread + short-sale constraint (Gay & Jung 1999); calendar spread fair value F*_far − F*_near; program 차익 net flow as the coincident arbitrage indicator.
- **Sources**: Shreve II Thm 5.6.2 p. 241, Thm 5.6.5 p. 245, eq. 5.6.11 p. 247, §5.5 pp. 234–239; Cornell & French 1983 JFM 3(1); MacKinlay & Ramaswamy 1988 RFS 1(2); Gay & Jung 1999 JFM 19(2); Ko 2022 KJFS 51(2); Mirae 2003 note (CD91 vs call rate); KIS `basis`/`hts_thpr`/`dprt` fields; KIS program-trade endpoints.
- **User-material refs**: Shreve II pp. 240–247; ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW §0 (dividends 5.5.6–5.5.8).
- **Data needs**: daily futures close/OI for front and next (KIS, ≤100 rows/call), KOSPI200 spot, CD91 and KOFR daily, dividend-point estimate (index dividend yield × December weight; UNVERIFIED keyless source), KRX holiday calendar; min history 2 years.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt (`inquire_daily_fuopchartprice`, `display_board_futures`), KIS-domestic-stock (index, program trade), other: KOFIA/BOK for rates.
- **Backtest design**: no directional trade; the "roll timing" test: at 5 trading days before expiry, roll to the next contract on the day the calendar spread is nearest its fair value versus rolling on a fixed day; cost 1 tick per leg (12,500 KRW) + 0.0079% commission; benchmark: fixed-day roll; universe: KOSPI200 front/next.
- **Pre-registered validation**: (a) reconciliation: |F*_app − hts_thpr| ≤ 0.1 index pt on ≥ 95% of days once the dividend and rate conventions are aligned; (b) mispricing m_t is mean-reverting with AR(1) coefficient < 0.9 at daily frequency and |m_t| exceeds the cost band on < 5% of days outside December; (c) roll-timing saves ≥ 1 tick per roll on average over ≥ 8 rolls with p < 0.10 (else the fixed-day roll stays the default).
- **Expected edge / decay**: near zero for a retail account (no basket execution); the value is the desk-language basis read and the December dividend-basis break (0.85–1.18%).
- **Complexity**: 2. **Caveats**: no verified KRX 이론가 formula; dividend-point data source; night-session (글로벌거래) basis differs.

### 5. Short delta-hedged straddle / synthetic short variance on KOSPI200 (volatility)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 monthly options hedged with KOSPI200 futures (mini futures for finer hedge); ES options via KIS overseas as the U.S. comparator.
- **Family**: volatility.
- **Mechanism**: sell the ATM straddle nearest to 30 days (or the 1/K² strip), delta-hedge daily at the close with Black-76 delta at σ_h = traded IV; daily P&L ≈ −½Γ S²(r_t²/Δ − σ_h²)Δ·(contracts) + carry − costs (the identity of candidate 10 with a short sign); roll at 7 days to expiry; size so that a 3σ index move costs ≤ 2% of equity.
- **Sources**: Carr & Wu 2009 RFS 22(3); Bakshi & Kapadia 2003 RFS 16(2); Yoon & Byun 2009 JFM 29(9) (no VRP in KOSPI200 — the null); Ahn, Kang & Ryu 2008 JFM 28(12) (adverse selection in spreads); Meritz 2024 commissions; Volmageddon (ETF.com; CFA FAJ) and March 2020 (Bloomberg) for the tail.
- **User-material refs**: paper §5.1 p. 11 (VRP = Cov^P(Z, RV)), §6.6 p. 15 (straddle premium ≈ 1.6 e^{−rT} S_0 √T (σ_Q − σ_P)), §6.7 p. 16 eq. (34), Prop. 1 (35); Shreve II p. 160 (gamma cost of the short), p. 162.
- **Data needs**: daily board snapshots (mid, IV, Greeks) for ≥ 3 years going forward (no KIS back history), daily futures, CD91; realised variance from KOSPI200 closes.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt; KIS-overseas-futopt (`opt_daily_ccnl` "OES…" for ES options, 120-row window) or yfinance SPX chain.
- **Backtest design**: signal = always short (unconditional) and a conditional variant short only when MFIV − trailing-21d RV > 0 (BTZ-style); daily rebalancing; cost = 0.35% of premium per side + half-spread (0.01/0.05 pt) + futures 0.0079% + 1 tick; benchmark: zero and long KOSPI200 futures; universe: front monthly.
- **Pre-registered validation**: mean daily P&L after costs > 0 with Newey–West t > 2.0 over ≥ 36 months, Sharpe ≥ 0.5, and the worst 21-day drawdown ≤ 6× the average monthly gain; if t ≤ 2.0 the Korean null (Yoon & Byun) is accepted and the strategy is shelved; the U.S. leg must show the Bakshi–Kapadia sign (negative delta-hedged long-option gains) in the same window as a sanity check.
- **Expected edge / decay**: U.S.: positive but crash-exposed (−46% month in Mar 2020 for short variance); Korea: possibly zero after the post-2012 shift to foreign-institution dominance; decays with crowding and rises in commissions.
- **Complexity**: 4. **Caveats**: needs a 선물옵션 account with the 2천만원 deposit tier for option selling and 10 trading days of prior open-interest experience; margin calls intraday; the app has no broker order path (README) — paper trading only.

### 6. Variance-risk-premium timing of index futures (BTZ) (volatility → directional)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 futures; ES.
- **Family**: volatility (as a return predictor).
- **Mechanism**: VRP_t = MFIV_t² − RV_{t−21,t}² (annualised); position in the index future proportional to the z-score of VRP over a 2-year window, capped at ±1; hold 21 days, rebalanced daily with overlapping tranches.
- **Sources**: Bollerslev, Tauchen & Zhou 2009 RFS 22(11) (quarterly-horizon predictability; requires model-free IV); Carr & Wu 2009.
- **User-material refs**: paper §5.1 p. 11 (VRP as a Z-moment), §7 (Sharpe bias of short-Z strategies, Table 5 D2); ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW §1.2 (drift study: sample means do not forecast — the VRP is a conditioning variable, not a drift).
- **Data needs**: MFIV daily (candidate 3 or VKOSPI/VIX), index closes; ≥ 5 years (VIX/VKOSPI history exists; KIS board history does not, so use the published indices for the backtest).
- **Reachable via**: yfinance (`^VIX`, `ES=F`, `^GSPC`), other: KRX VKOSPI; KIS-futopt for live.
- **Backtest design**: signal on close t, earn t+1; cost 1 tick + 0.0079% per rebalance; benchmark buy-and-hold futures; universe KOSPI200 and S&P 500.
- **Pre-registered validation**: out-of-sample (expanding window from 2015) predictive regression of 63-day returns on VRP: slope > 0 with t > 2 and OOS R² > 0 (Campbell–Thompson sense) in at least one of the two markets; the trading rule's Sharpe ≥ buy-and-hold + 0.2 after costs; failure in Korea is reported, not tuned away.
- **Expected edge / decay**: documented for the U.S. 1990–2007; weaker with daily-data RV; unknown for Korea.
- **Complexity**: 2. **Caveats**: overlapping-return inference (use Hodrick or Newey–West with 63 lags); VKOSPI history access.

### 7. VIX futures roll-down / basis trade (carry)
- **Asset class**: VIX futures (CFE) hedged with ES; proxy via `^VIX`/`^VIX3M`.
- **Family**: carry.
- **Mechanism**: when the front VIX future trades above spot VIX (contango) by more than a threshold, short the front future and hedge the equity beta with a long ES position sized by the regression beta of VIX-future changes on ES returns; the carry is the roll-down toward spot as expiry nears.
- **Sources**: Simon & Campasano 2014 J. Derivatives 21(3) 54–69 (basis does not predict VIX changes; hedged trade profitable); Simon 2017 JFM; Volmageddon sources for the tail (VIX +115.6% on 5 Feb 2018).
- **User-material refs**: paper §5.5 term structure p. 13 and F9 "carry as short variance" (Table 5 p. 19).
- **Data needs**: VIX futures settlements (Cboe historical CSVs, keyless — UNVERIFIED in this session), `^VIX`, `ES=F`; ≥ 8 years including 2018 and 2020.
- **Reachable via**: yfinance (spot indices); other: Cboe CSV; KIS-overseas-futopt UNVERIFIED for CFE products.
- **Backtest design**: daily; enter when basis > 5% of spot, exit at 2% or at 5 days to expiry; cost US$1.50 commission + 1 tick (0.05 = US$50) per side; benchmark: unhedged short front future; universe: front and second VIX futures.
- **Pre-registered validation**: mean daily P&L > 0 with t > 2 after costs, Sharpe ≥ 0.6, and survival of the 5 Feb 2018 and Mar 2020 windows with a drawdown ≤ 30% of the prior 12-month gain under the stated stop; the unhedged benchmark must show the larger drawdown (otherwise the hedge design is wrong).
- **Expected edge / decay**: documented 2006–2012; more crowded since ETP growth; tail is the strategy.
- **Complexity**: 3. **Caveats**: not executable from the Korean account without a verified CFE route; keep as a model/monitor with a paper P&L.

### 8. Implied-vs-realised volatility forecast evaluation (model; feeds the Quant Lab)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200; S&P 500.

### 9. Option-implied skew / call–put IV spread as a daily index predictor (signal)
- **Asset class**: KOSPI200 (futures as the instrument); S&P 500.
- **Family**: flow / structural.
- **Mechanism**: signal_t = (IV_call − IV_put) at the same strike and expiry (volatility spread, Nam et al.) or the 25Δ risk reversal from the SVI fit; secondary: the spot–futures basis, which Nam et al. find is the fundamental driver; predict 1–5 day KOSPI200 returns; trade the future with position = sign × min(|z|, 1).
- **Sources**: Nam, Lee, Kang & Choi (KDA working paper; Kim 2011 cited therein) — Korean evidence; Xing, Zhang & Zhao 2010 JFQA 45(3) — cross-sectional analogue, not index; Ahn–Kang–Ryu 2008 (informed trading in KOSPI200 options).
- **User-material refs**: paper §5.2 skew premium p. 12, §3.5; Table 5 E7.
- **Data needs**: daily board snapshots (front expiry), futures and spot closes; ≥ 2 years of snapshots (must be accumulated).
- **Reachable via**: KIS-futopt; yfinance for SPX.
- **Backtest design**: signal at close t, earn t+1 to t+5; cost 1 tick + 0.0079% per trade; benchmark: the basis-only signal and buy-and-hold; universe: KOSPI200 front future.
- **Pre-registered validation**: predictive regression t > 2 OOS on ≥ 500 days AND incremental R² over the basis-only regression > 0.5 percentage points; otherwise the IV spread is dropped and the basis signal alone is kept as a candidate 4 sub-feature.
- **Expected edge / decay**: Korean papers report significance in older samples; likely small after costs; decays with market-maker efficiency.
- **Complexity**: 2. **Caveats**: no back history from KIS; parity-implied spread mechanically equals the basis unless the spot–futures basis is used as the underlying (Nam et al. §4-2, 4-3).

### 10. Hedge P&L simulation for the structuring pages (model)
- **Asset class**: any equity-index-linked note leg (PPN call, reverse-convertible put, autocall — the last is not yet in the app); KOSPI200 or S&P 500 underlying.
- **Family**: structural.
- **Mechanism**: GARCH-t P-paths (bounded innovations, seeded) with drift mode "premium"/"zero"/"rf"; Black-76 Q-pricer at σ_h; daily delta hedge; Π_T = payoff − Σ hedge gains − e^{rT}V_0(σ_h); decomposition Π_T ≈ ½Σ e^{r(T−t_i)}Γ_i S_i²(r_i²/Δ − σ_h²)Δ + discretisation; break-even realised vol; CVaR of Π_T in bp of notional.
- **Sources**: user's paper §6.7 eq. (34) p. 16 and Prop. 1 (35); Shreve II §4.5.5 pp. 159–160, p. 162, §5.2.4 p. 218; ARCHITECTURE_REVIEW §1.3 (martingale property of the rf mode).
- **User-material refs**: as listed; Table 5 E5 p. 19; formula sheet p. 19.
- **Data needs**: the underlying's 2-year daily history (KIS/yfinance) for the GARCH fit; σ_h from candidate 1/2 or a user input; CD91/SOFR.
- **Reachable via**: KIS-domestic-stock (KOSPI200 via index endpoint), yfinance; KIS-futopt for σ_h.
- **Backtest design**: n/a (simulation); acceptance tests in `tests/`.
- **Pre-registered validation**: (i) GBM paths, σ_h = σ_true: |mean Π_T| < 1 standard error and std(Π_T) ∝ N^{−1/2} across N ∈ {21, 63, 252} (log-log slope in [−0.6, −0.4]); (ii) GBM with σ_h ≠ σ_true: regression of Π_T on the discrete identity has slope ∈ [0.9, 1.1], R² > 0.9; (iii) GARCH-t rf mode: mean e^{−rT}Π_T within one 5,000-path standard error of 0 (Prop. 1); (iv) the sign of mean Π_T equals sign(E[σ_t²] − σ_h²) under P.
- **Expected edge / decay**: none; it prices the hedge risk of the pitchbook products in bp of notional and answers OTM question E5.
- **Complexity**: 3. **Caveats**: transaction costs of the daily hedge (add 1 tick per rebalance as an option); jumps (GARCH-t has fat tails but no jumps; the Merton model in the app can be a second engine).
