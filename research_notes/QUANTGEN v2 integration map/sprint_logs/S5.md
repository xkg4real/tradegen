## Gotchas for the next implementers

- M8's data step builds `CardInputs` from the PIT store once the fixtures are listed: `books = cardp.build_books(m_earning, cardp.excess_returns(...), quote.bp,
  dates)` with `quote = instruments.cost_per_side(book, price, on=)`; `closes` are `official_close` rows only; the control is `cardp.lookahead_positive_control(
  cardp.timing_probe(<KR_SOX_KRW_gate_B3>, sandbox=True, ...))` called from docs/ or tests/; `series` = `ledger.difference_series(loader)`; `neighbours` are the
  eight `gate.card_p_v2.pc6_axes` runs; `snapshot_rows` the PIT rows behind `data_snapshot_id`. Then `run_card(..., inputs=)` after a real freeze.
- A header commit needs the card's own configuration registered first (`N_raw_at_registration >= 1`): register the research-phase rows of the family
  (seed files) and the card's configuration through `gate_ledger.register_trial` before `--commit-headers`.
- `step3` needs `market` and a date `data_span.start` on the card it receives; the committed header has neither (`market` lives on `CardSpec`,
  `data_span.start` is the warm-up convention), so pass `{**header, "market": spec.market, "data_span": {"start": books.dates[0], ...}}` as the runner does.
- `evaluate_card` is ~40 `cardp_evaluate` calls (band 32 + halves 2 + regime 4 + full span + 8 neighbours by the caller): at B = 2,000 on 7,700 sessions that
  is minutes per card (freeze tier); B = 20 runs in 35 s for a seam test.
- `card_fields` drops what the closed schema does not declare (list above); extend `core/ficc/schemas/model_card.macro.v2.0.json` (an M0 package item,
  versioned) before writing a new printed line onto a card.
- `gate_package.package_state(store, macro_dir)` is the one package-state reader (BLOCKED needs the macro dir); `cards.package_frozen` delegates to it.
- Run suites one at a time; `python -m tests.macro_acceptance` last.
