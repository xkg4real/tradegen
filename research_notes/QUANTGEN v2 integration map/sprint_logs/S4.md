   books.neutralise_rule(reg, rid, eid, dec))`, reload the registry, the market_gross record carries the flagged multiplier at 1.0 and `overlay_min` 0.75; without the
   carrier the factor still acts (0.5). The `runtime[]` entry's key set is unchanged (the carrier is not an artifact field).
2. **Ladder and cap into composition, evaluator and ledger.** Per decision and book: `res = books.risk_step(reg, bid, dec, nav)` -> `ladder, block =
   risk_layer.ladder_for_book(res.ladder, bid, binding_share=<frozen share or None>)` -> `sleeves.apply_market_gross(book, vt, overlays, ladder=ladder,
   post_rearm_cap=books.post_rearm_cap(res.book), concentration_bound=cb.bound)` -> `evaluator.evaluator_block("R-31", decision, BlockState(block_new_entries=block,
   market, w_held, w_target))` -> `books.record_decision(reg, bid, dec, target, fillable=<simulate_fill>, block=block)` -> `books.step_decision(...)` last. Asserted:
   0.8 x min(1, 0.5, 1) = 0.4 with and without an overlay at 0.5 (never 0.2); BLOCKED only for a target above held; the ledger holds the blocked entry; after the hard stop
   HALTED is 0x outside min() (`by_book` zeros, `hard_stop: rearm_refused`); after the automatic re-arm `post_rearm_cap(book) == 0.5` gives 0.4 = vt x 0.5 with
   `post_rearm_cap` in the binding log and `hard_stop: post_rearm_cap` in the state; the governance chain is `book_state, halt, rearm`.
