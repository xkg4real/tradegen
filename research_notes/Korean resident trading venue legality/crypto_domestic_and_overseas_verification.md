# Verification: crypto-asset trading for a Korean resident (domestic VASPs, overseas exchanges, DEX)

Adversarial fact-check of `crypto_domestic_and_overseas.md`, run 2026-10-23. These notes describe what statutes, regulator releases and platform terms say. They are not legal advice.

**Method.** The checker read primary sources where they could be fetched:
- Statute text of the Virtual Asset User Protection Act (가상자산이용자보호법, Act No. 19563) Art. 10, 17 and 19, and 특금법 Art. 7 (current version), via casenote.kr mirrors of the statute, because law.go.kr returns a JS shell.
- FSC press releases 2025-09-05 (85247), 2026-06-24 (87177) and 2026-08-11 (87499).
- Upbit's official API docs.
- Hyperliquid's live Terms of Use JS bundle (last updated 2026-06-15).
- pump.fun Terms (last updated 2026-10-17), including §32.
- OKX's Risk & Compliance Disclosure.
- Hyperliquid's official docs (fees, margin tiers).

The web-search budget ran out before checks on these were finished: Bybit and Binance restricted-jurisdiction lists, the current Bithumb minimum order, Gopax's renewal filing date, and any October 2026 movement on the Digital Asset Basic Act. Those items are marked uncertain.

## What the researcher missed

**1. User-side criminal exposure under 특금법.** The Supreme Court (2024도10710) holds that someone who repeatedly buys, sells or transfers crypto for other people for a fee is an unregistered VASP. That is criminal under Art. 17(1): up to 5 years or KRW 50M. Examples: kimchi-premium arbitrage run for friends, P2P/OTC brokering, or running a copy-trading pool. So TRADEGEN must never handle other people's money. The User Protection Act's manipulation bans (10(2)–(4)) also bind ordinary users, and the penalty is imprisonment *or* a fine, not both by default.

**2. Venue and age restrictions in the platforms' own terms.**
- OKX's own disclosure restricts derivatives and P2P for South Korea, so "Binance/OKX/Bybit perps" cannot be treated as one grey bucket.
- Hyperliquid's §1.7 shuts outcome (prediction) markets to designated or unlawful jurisdictions.
- pump.fun's age clause ties to the age of majority, which is 19 in Korea.
- The minimum age for KR exchange accounts was not checked.

**3. Timing.** The decree's travel-rule tightening is not yet in force; it takes effect about February 2027. The FX Act cross-border regime starts 2026-12-03. The tax start (2027-01-01) is still live pending the November vote. The FSC has also discouraged Korean brokers from selling overseas spot BTC ETFs since 2024.
