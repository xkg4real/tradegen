# Verification: Korean domestic securities and exchange-traded derivatives (~KRW 42,000, Korean resident)

Checked 2026-10-23 against `domestic_securities_derivatives.md`. This describes what rules and reporting say and is not legal advice.

Method limits:
- law.go.kr pages render through JavaScript and returned no text, so statute text came from the casenote.kr mirror. That mirror can lag behind amendments; for example, it shows FSCMA Art. 180 at its 2021 version, although the FSCMA was amended on 2024-10-22.
- KRX and KOFIA rulebooks were not opened.
- The shared web-search budget ran out partway through. Items marked "not verified" were not settled.

## What the researcher missed

**Venue table fixes:**
- **KONEX row:** the basic deposit was abolished on 2022-05-30. KONEX should be "legal" and reachable, with a ±15% limit.
- **Credit row:** max ≈2.2x at the 45% floor, not 2.5x.
- **Short-selling row:** reachability should be "unclear".
- **Overseas leveraged products:** they now carry deposits of KRW 10M (general) or KRW 30M cash (single-stock).

**Reachability:** domestic fractional-share trading starts at **KRW 1,000** at several brokers, for example KakaoPay Securities from 2026-08-10 and iM Securities from 2026-10-06 (KOSPI200 and KOSDAQ150 names only, batched fills) ([SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000338432); [CEO Score Daily](https://www.ceoscoredaily.com/page/view/2026081016291959765)). This makes high-priced shares reachable with KRW 42,000.

**Market-surveillance rules:**
- KRX market warnings move from caution (투자주의) to warning (투자경고) to risk (투자위험), with trading halts. Credit is banned on warning and risk stocks.
- In 2026, KRX dropped the 100% cash-margin requirement for warning and risk stocks ([SBS Biz](https://biz.sbs.co.kr/amp/article/20000335715); [KIND](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2026/07/15/000716/20260715001881/68818.htm)).
- These rules, more than the ±30% limit, are what stop consecutive limit-up runs.

**Pending regulatory changes:** both affect an aggressive retail account in the next month.
- **Single-stock leverage:** the 20% portfolio cap on single-stock leverage is still pending. Officials have also floated cutting the 2x multiple or limiting new buys to professional investors.
- **Credit:** KOFIA's 50% minimum credit-deposit rate is planned within 2026.

**Still unresolved:**
- whether 2x ETFs carry a multiplied price limit;
- current retail short-selling education, mock-trading and borrowing limits;
- the KRX/금융투자업규정 article numbers for price limits, VI, the derivatives deposit and the FX margin floor;
- whether the NXT VI change and the 20-unit lot took effect.
