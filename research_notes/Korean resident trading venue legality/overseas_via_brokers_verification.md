## What the researcher missed

The biggest miss is the 2026 tightening of leveraged products. From **2026-05-22**, overseas-listed leveraged ETPs need a **KRW 10M 기본예탁금** for anyone without prior overseas leveraged-ETP trades. From **2026-07-31**, all single-stock leveraged and inverse ETPs, domestic or overseas, need **KRW 30M in cash**, including for additional buys, plus a 3-hour course. That takes TQQQ/SOXL-type products, and every single-stock 2x product, out of reach for a KRW 42,000 account. The FSC also froze new single-stock listings and banned their advertising (2026-07-16).

Second, user-side conduct is regulated through the **FX rules**, not just through 자본시장법:
- 외국환거래규정 제7-33조④ directly obliges 일반투자가 to trade foreign securities through a 투자중개업자.
- Skipping that route triggers BOK reporting (제7-31조②) and FX-bank reporting for overseas deposits (제7-11조②).
- Non-reporting carries administrative fines of up to KRW 100M (외국환거래법 제32조①4호), or a warning for small sums.
- 자본시장법 itself has no penalty provision tied to 제166조 or 시행령 제184조①.

Third, a pending amendment to 자본시장법 제166조 (법률 제21324호, 2026-02-03, in force one year after promulgation, so about 2027-02) adds 일반투자자 OTC-trade amount limits and credit limits. It is worth tracking for any OTC or CFD route.

Finally, two smaller points. Toss appears to have options live in 2026, buy-only, with no exercise and no margin. Foreign and domestic share gains are netted in one tax bucket rather than taxed separately.
