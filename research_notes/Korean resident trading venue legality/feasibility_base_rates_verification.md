## What the researcher missed

**Venue table changes.** The researcher's venue table needs three changes:
- **Overseas leveraged ETPs** have required a KRW 10M basic deposit for first-time buyers since 22 May 2026, so they are **not** reachable with KRW 42,000.
- **Single-stock leveraged ETF/ETN** need KRW 30M in cash, 3h of education and 5-day mock trading (from 31 Jul / 19 Aug 2026).
- **US single-stock and 0DTE options** are offered to Korean retail by domestic brokers. Mark them legal_with_conditions (1h + 3h), not unknown.

**User-side criminal exposure was understated.** For prediction markets and sports betting:
- 형법 제3조 plus 대법원 2002도2518 settle that gambling abroad by a Korean national is punishable.
- Habitual gambling (형법 246②) carries up to 3 years.
- For any sports-outcome contract, **국민체육진흥법 제48조 제3호 sets up to 5 years or KRW 50M for the bettor**, not only a KRW 10M fine.

By contrast, no user-side penalty was found for merely holding an account on an unregistered offshore VASP. The exposure there is access blocking and withdrawal risk.

**The supermartingale bound needs limited liability.** It holds only when losses are capped at the account balance. Products where losses can exceed the account (overseas futures) break the bound by adding debt risk, not edge.

**Not researched at all:**
- The legal state lottery and sports betting (동행복권 Lotto 6/45, 베트맨/스포츠토토). These are the only clearly lawful lottery-like payoffs. Lotto first-prize odds are 1 in C(45,6) = 8,145,060.
- Tax: overseas-stock and derivatives capital-gains rules, and the 1 Jan 2027 crypto tax start.
