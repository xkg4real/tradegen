## What the researcher missed

The most important miss is that **Korean police are already prosecuting prediction-market users, not just blocking the site.** The 강원경찰청 사이버수사대 opened an inquiry on 2026-03-16 and booked 26 Polymarket users. By mid-September it had referred 18 to prosecutors without detention on gambling charges under 형법 제246조. Users were identified by OSINT analysis of public blockchain records. The police position is that staking crypto on an uncertain outcome is gambling "regardless of the 'investment' label". Defence counsel argue the activity is crypto-derivatives investment, and no court has ruled yet. Reported stakes were large (KRW 17.6bn in total, KRW 5.7bn for the largest bettor), and no source says whether small-stake users were booked. Sources: [강원일보 2026-09-18](https://www.kwnews.co.kr/article/20260917501775), [Chosun Biz via Daum 2026-09-08](https://v.daum.net/v/20260908104207366), [The Block 2026-09-17](https://theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-09-17-south-korean-police-charge-polymarket-users-415333).

Three other gaps follow:

- **자본시장법 시행령 제184조①** (requires a domestic 투자중개업자 for overseas exchange-traded derivatives) was not analysed for Kalshi. Kalshi's own Member Agreement §VI makes eligibility depend on trading not being "prohibited under Applicable Law" where the member is located.
- **국민체육진흥법 제26조②** (current version effective 2026-06-02) separately bars anyone from designing, building or offering systems for similar acts, supplying match information for them, or brokering or promoting purchases. Any TRADEGEN feature that routes, signals or brokers sports-contract bets would create tool-builder exposure beyond user-side 제48조3호 risk.
- **Taxes and FX.** 외국환거래법 reporting and tax treatment of offshore winnings were not covered.
