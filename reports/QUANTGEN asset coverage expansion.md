# One KIS key unlocks six markets read-only

The app key and secret QUANTGEN already holds reach every KIS asset class the question names without an account number: the official `koreainvestment/open-trading-api` repository (commit b4e6249, 26 Aug 2026) exposes **199 quotation and reference REST endpoints plus 60 websocket feeds that never send `CANO`**, covering domestic stocks and ETFs (108 + 5 endpoints), ELWs (21), index, KTB and FX futures and options (9), overseas stocks (28), overseas futures and options (20) and exchange-traded bonds (8), each with daily history paged 30 to 120 rows per call ([examples_llm parse](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/tree/main/examples_llm)). Two cheap probe calls, one KOSPI200 futures quote and one NASDAQ quote, settle the only open prerequisite question, because nothing in the official material says a derivatives account or overseas agreement gates *quotations* as opposed to orders. The daily-frequency models that clear Korean retail costs are the structural, trend and carry families (Faber/MOP trend switches, capped volatility targeting, Avellaneda–Zhang leveraged-ETF drag, Black-76 board inversion with SVI and model-free variance, Diebold–Li curve forecasts, PCA-neutral butterflies, Koijen et al. carry), while ETF premium reversal, same-index pairs and KOSPI200 short-straddles either have no documented Korean edge (**KODEX 200's price error averages −0.237% and is not autocorrelated beyond one to three days; Yoon and Byun find no priced volatility risk in KOSPI200 options**) or need option-chain history that KIS does not archive. Crypto never comes from KIS: the FSC's January 2024 stance still blocks brokers from overseas spot bitcoin ETFs, CME bitcoin futures have been barred since December 2017, and the domestic spot-ETF bill was still a proposal on 1 September 2026, so the read-only route is **Upbit's keyless daily candles (KRW-BTC from 2017-09-25, 200 per call) plus Binance's keyless klines and 8-hourly funding (from 2017-08-17 and 2019-09-10)**, with an optional Upbit 자산조회-only key for balances ahead of the 22% virtual-asset tax that starts 1 January 2027. Outside KIS and wallets only two channels earn a build, CFE VIX futures (on KIS's overseas-futures menu, Cboe settlements keyless from 2004) and FX momentum, dollar-carry and value extensions of the existing carry basket; IBKR is a regulatory grey zone for individuals, domestic FX margin has shrunk by more than 70% and KIS has exited it, prediction markets were declared gambling in August 2026, and KAU carbon stays closed to individuals until at least 2028. Admission to the updated app runs through one gate: purged walk-forward (IS 756 / OOS 126 sessions), a block-bootstrap Sharpe confidence interval excluding zero, deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95, probability of backtest overfitting ≤ 0.05, survival at twice assumed costs and a parameter plateau for strategies; Kupiec, Christoffersen and PIT–KS tests over at least 500 origins for distributional models; a PASS / REVISE / REJECT / INSUFFICIENT DATA verdict written to a model card. The final section ranks 55 candidates inside seven asset-class groups and pins each to the user's own textbooks, lecture decks and papers by section and printed page.

## One app key already serves 199 quotation endpoints across six KIS domains

The audit parsed all 333 `examples_llm/<domain>/<api>/<api>.py` files for `API_URL`, `tr_id`, request parameters and whether `CANO` is sent, and found **201 REST endpoints that need no account number (199 quotation/reference plus two auth), 73 REST endpoints that send `CANO` (all under `/trading/` paths) and 60 websocket feeds**; per domain the no-CANO counts are domestic_stock 108, etfetn 5, elw 21, domestic_futureoption 9, overseas_stock 28, overseas_futureoption 20 and domestic_bond 8 ([README](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/README.md); [convention](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/docs/convention.md)). Every read-only call carries the same header set, `authorization: Bearer <token>`, `appkey`, `appsecret`, `tr_id`, `custtype: P`, and an optional `tr_cont` for continuation ([kis_auth.py](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_user/kis_auth.py)). The token comes from `POST /oauth2/tokenP` with `client_credentials`, lives one day, is re-issued at most once per minute (a second request within a minute returns error EGW00133), and a re-request within six hours returns the same token while every issuance triggers a KakaoTalk notice; the official client paces real-account calls at 20 per second and mock calls at 2 per second, with EGW00201 signalling "초당 거래건수 초과" ([README §5](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/README.md); [kis_auth.py](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_user/kis_auth.py)). QUANTGEN's `data/krx/kis_client.py` already caches the token on disk with its expiry and refuses to mint while a cached one is valid, which protects against the throttle and the notification noise rather than against invalidation. Base URLs are `https://openapi.koreainvestment.com:9443` for real and `https://openapivts.koreainvestment.com:29443` for mock, with websockets on `ws://ops.koreainvestment.com:21000` and `:31000` ([kis_devlp.yaml](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/kis_devlp.yaml)). Three facts about codes decide the implementation. First, **KRX derivatives short codes changed their product-type letter for 2026-plus maturities**: futures `1→A`, calls `2→B`, puts `3→C`, spreads `4→D`, because 12-character standard codes repeat on a 30-year cycle, so `1016C` became `A016C` for KOSPI200 December 2026 ([유안타증권 공지](https://www.myasset.com/myasset/customer/notice/CU_0201000_P2.cmd?SEQ=202301160940590000000004&gubun=norNotice); [유진투자선물 공지](https://www.eugenefutures.com/corebbs/CU0700/view.cmd?bbsSeq=769)). The year character is a 30-symbol cycle (S = 2022, T = 2023, V = 2024, W = 2025, 6 = 2026) reconstructed from broker pages and the live master rather than an official table, so the app must read `단축코드`, `행사가`, `월물구분코드` and `기초자산` from `fo_idx_code_mts.mst` and `fo_com_code.mst` and select the front month by `월물구분코드 = 1`, never derive a code ([fo_idx_code_mts.mst](https://new.real.download.dws.co.kr/common/master/fo_idx_code_mts.mst.zip); [domestic_index_future_code.py](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/stocks_info/domestic_index_future_code.py)). Second, the overseas-futures master carries 5,021 rows and 477 product codes across CME (3,236), SGX, ICE, EUREX, HKEx, CBOE and OSE, with tick size, tick value, contract size and nearest-month flags, so contract specs never need to be typed ([ffcode.mst](https://new.real.download.dws.co.kr/common/master/ffcode.mst.zip); [overseas_future_code.py](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/stocks_info/overseas_future_code.py)). Third, the overseas-index master (12,919 rows) is the only KIS route to a daily 원/달러 fixing (`XFX@KRW`), Korean benchmark yields (`RY0101` 국고채 3년, `RY0102` 회사채 AA−) and COMEX gold (`EM0101`), which overlaps FRED and Yahoo but adds the KRW series the Macro page lacks; the exact market-code-plus-code mapping for `inquire-daily-chartprice` beyond the tested `N` + `.DJI` is an inference to confirm with one call each ([frgn_code.mst](https://new.real.download.dws.co.kr/common/master/frgn_code.mst.zip); [chk_inquire_daily_chartprice](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/overseas_stock/inquire_daily_chartprice/chk_inquire_daily_chartprice.py)).

History depth is undocumented everywhere; the repository states per-call caps but never an archive floor. The working evidence is QUANTGEN's own 504-session KRX window, which implies multi-year daily depth for stocks and, by the same design, for indices and futures front-month codes; a futures code only exists for its listed life, so long histories need a continuous-contract stitch at roll, and whether expired option series remain queryable through `inquire-daily-fuopchartprice` is unverified. Minute data is shallower by design: domestic stock minute bars keep one year ("최대 1년 분봉 보관") at 120 rows per call, domestic F/O minute bars page at 102, overseas stock minutes at 120 with `KEYB` cursors ([inquire_time_dailychartprice](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/domestic_stock/inquire_time_dailychartprice/inquire_time_dailychartprice.py)). For a daily app the websocket layer adds nothing REST does not deliver at the close; its only exclusive series are the bond-index tick stream `H0BICNT0` and intraday KRX night-session prices, and it brings AES-CBC frame decryption, a 40-subscription cap and PINGPONG handling that QUANTGEN does not need ([KISWebSocket](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_user/kis_auth.py); [bond_index_ccnl](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/domestic_bond/bond_index_ccnl/bond_index_ccnl.py)).

On account prerequisites the answer splits cleanly between quoting and trading. **None of the 199 quotation endpoints sends an account number**; the only identity fields they ever need are the app key, secret and token, plus an HTS ID for the four personal watch-list and condition-search endpoints ([psearch_title](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/domestic_stock/psearch_title/psearch_title.py)). Trading is different: the config template keeps separate 8-digit stems for a stock account and a 선물옵션 account with product suffixes `01` 종합, `03` 국내선물옵션 and `08` 해외선물옵션, and KIS's service prerequisites are "계좌 개설 및 ID 연결 → Open API 서비스 신청 → 앱키/앱시크릿 발급" ([kis_devlp.yaml](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/kis_devlp.yaml); [README §3.4–3.5](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/README.md)). Because quotation requests carry no account field, the gateway can only gate them at the app-key level, and no official statement was found that a derivatives account or overseas agreement is required merely to query; the user's key was issued against a stock account, so the cheapest resolution is one `inquire-price` call with `F`/`A01612` and one overseas `price` call with `NAS`/`AAPL`. If the user later wants to *trade* KOSPI200 or KTB futures, the 2019-12-02 retail regime applies: a 기본예탁금 of at least 1천만원 for futures and option buying and 2천만원 for everything else including option writing, 사전교육 of at least one hour and 모의거래 of at least three hours, and for option selling and VKOSPI futures ten trading days of prior open-interest history ([Meritz 거래설명서 p. 10](https://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/bbs/contents/bascGrp/help13/20241111/yk24111102.pdf); [Yuanta 거래설명서 pp. 3, 15](https://www.myasset.com/myasset/static/notice/250605_03.pdf)). Overseas F/O at KIS additionally requires the 모의거래 이수 course, and overseas stock quotes are labelled "실시간지연체결가" on the websocket, so they are delayed unless a paid entitlement exists ([delayed_ccnl](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/overseas_stock/delayed_ccnl/delayed_ccnl.py)).

What KIS does not give read-only matters as much as what it does. There is no investor-type endpoint for futures or options (the stock-side `inquire-investor-daily-by-market` covers indices only), so derivatives positioning must come from the KRX data marketplace or pykrx; there is no REST bond-index or benchmark-curve endpoint in the bond module, so KRW benchmark yields come from the stock module's `comp-interest` whose division-code values the sample does not document; the market code that the F/O daily endpoint expects for KTB, USD and gold futures is not shown in any sample (only `F`, `O` and `JF`), so one probe decides whether the Rates and FX pages can source KTB and USD futures from KIS; and the option board's `hts_ints_vltl` and `hts_thpr` are of unstated construction (which underlying, rate and day count), which is why the first options model below is a reconciliation rather than a trade ([inquire_daily_fuopchartprice](https://github.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/blob/main/examples_llm/domestic_futureoption/inquire_daily_fuopchartprice/inquire_daily_fuopchartprice.py); [chk_display_board_callput](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/main/examples_llm/domestic_futureoption/display_board_callput/chk_display_board_callput.py)).

## Daily models that clear Korean retail costs cluster in trend, carry and structure, not premium reversal

The cost model is the first filter, and it is asymmetric across venues. On the KIS BanKIS tariff a KRX ETF trades at **0.0146527% per side, so a flagship-ETF round trip is about 3 bp of commission plus one or two ticks of spread, roughly 4–5 bp, with no securities-transaction tax on ETF sells**; a US ETF or stock through KIS costs 0.25% per side plus a 0.00206% SEC fee on sells and two currency conversions at a 60–80% 우대 rate, roughly 55–130 bp per round trip before the 22% capital-gains tax on annual gains above ₩2.5 million ([KIS 수수료안내](https://securities.koreainvestment.com/main/customer/guide/_static/TF04ae010000.jsp); [KIS 해외주식 매매안내](https://securities.koreainvestment.com/main/bond/research/_static/TF03ca050000.jsp); [KB ETF 세금](https://kbthink.com/etf/etf-tax.html)). Domestic-equity ETFs pay no tax on gains but 15.4% on distributions; KRX-listed ETFs holding overseas stocks, bonds or commodities pay 15.4% on gains as well ([KB ETF 세금](https://kbthink.com/etf/etf-tax.html)). A KRX single name carries the securities-transaction tax on sells, which a secondary source puts at 0.20% for both KOSPI and KOSDAQ from 1 January 2026 after the abolition of the financial-investment-income tax; this figure must be confirmed against 증권거래세법 시행령 before it is hard-coded, and with it a 70,000-won stock round trip is about 0.37% and a two-leg pair about 0.74% ([Namu wiki 증권거래세](https://namu.wiki/w/증권거래세)). KOSPI200 derivatives are cheap in notional terms (online 0.007863% for index futures) but options are charged on premium, 0.395554% or 0.295554% per side, and KTB futures cost 0.002–0.011% of notional or a flat ₩3,000 per contract ([Meritz p. 7](https://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/bbs/contents/bascGrp/help13/20241111/yk24111102.pdf); [Yuanta pp. 22–23](https://www.myasset.com/myasset/static/notice/250605_03.pdf); [Shinhan pp. 9–10](https://file.shinhansec.com/filedoc/clause/k_6.pdf)). The pre-registered defaults that follow are 5 bp for KRX ETFs, 35 bp per side for KRX stocks including tax, 100 bp per round trip for US instruments via KIS, and every candidate is reported at one and two times these numbers.

### ETFs: harvest the structure, not the 괴리율

The ETF premium literature is strong in the US and weak where the user would trade. Petajisto finds ETF prices deviate from NAV "typically within a band of about 200 bps", about 100 bps after controlling for stale NAVs with a peer group of similar funds, with a long-discount/short-premium rule earning a Carhart alpha of roughly 11% per year before costs and positions converging "at a rate of 10 bp per day"; but the profits come from international and illiquid-underlying funds, and diversified US equity ETFs earn "low and occasionally even negative returns" ([CFA RPC summary](https://rpc.cfainstitute.org/research/financial-analysts-journal/2017/inefficiencies-in-the-pricing-of-exchange-traded-funds); [etf28.pdf pp. 5, 21–22](http://www.petajisto.net/papers/etf28.pdf)). The Korean evidence says the same about the flagship trackers: across eight KOSPI200 ETFs from 2002 to 2017 the price error (P − NAV)/NAV has a significant negative mean for every fund, largest for KODEX 200 at −0.237%, the absolute 괴리율 sits "대략 1% 수준" and is shrinking, and the error is not autocorrelated except for the two oldest funds, where it persists "1일 혹은 3일까지" ([서기수·엄윤성, KDA](https://www.kdajdqs.org/bbs/presentation/578/download/1027)). Marshall, Nguyen and Visaltanachoti show same-index SPY–IVV divergences correct in a median 2.27 and 0.92 minutes ([JBF 2013](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S037842661300232X)). A daily 괴리율-reversal rule on KODEX 200 or TIGER 200 therefore has expected gross edge of single-digit basis points against a 4–5 bp cost and is pre-registered to fail; the testable version is cross-sectional and peer-adjusted across the less liquid KRX ETFs (overseas-index, bond, commodity) where NAVs are struck on a foreign close, and even there the long-only form (buy the most discounted sibling when the exposure is wanted anyway) is an execution-cost saver rather than an alpha. The inputs exist twice over: KIS `nav-comparison-daily-trend` and the installed pykrx's `get_etf_price_deviation`, `get_etf_tracking_error` and `get_etf_portfolio_deposit_file`, though pykrx printed a KRX login warning at import and its keyless status must be tested ([pykrx](https://github.com/sharebook-kr/pykrx)).

Leveraged and inverse ETFs are the one place where the structure is exact. Avellaneda and Zhang derive L_t/L_0 = (S_t/S_0)^β · exp{((1−β)r − f)t + β∫λ ds + ((β − β²)/2)∫σ² ds}, so **a +2× fund loses one times, and a −2× fund three times, the integrated variance of the underlying**, before the fee and financing ([SIAM JFM 2010 pp. 590–592](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/SIAMLETFS.pdf)). With the app's GARCH(1,1)-t term structure the expected drag over N sessions is ((β² − β)/2)·Σσ̂²_{T+i} plus ((β−1)r + f)·N/252, which at 20% annualised volatility is about 400 bp a year for KODEX 레버리지 and 1,200 bp for KODEX 200선물인버스2X before their 0.64% fees ([FunETF KODEX 레버리지](https://www.funetf.co.kr/product/etf/view/KR7122630007)). Shorting KRX ETFs is not available to individuals in any verified form (개인대주 launched in 2021 for KOSPI200/KOSDAQ150 constituents and ETF eligibility is unverified), so the implementable use is a forecast tile and a cheapest-exposure selector, not a variance-harvesting pair ([FSC 개인대주](https://www.fsc.go.kr/po010101/75775)).

The trend and allocation rules carry the strongest evidence. Moskowitz, Ooi and Pedersen find "every single futures contract exhibits positive predictability from past one-year returns" across 58 instruments 1985–2009, size positions to a 40% ex-ante volatility using an EWMA with a 60-day centre of mass, and report a diversified Sharpe above one; Faber's 10-month SMA rule keeps the S&P 500 invested about 70% of the time with fewer than one round trip per year and would have cut the 1929–32 drawdown from 83.66% to 42.24% ([MOP, NYU PDF pp. 6–9](https://w4.stern.nyu.edu/facdir/lpederse/papers/TimeSeriesMomentum.pdf); [Faber 2013 update pp. 21–24](https://mebfaber.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/05/SSRN-id962461.pdf)). Both rules are expressible in `core/quant/backtest.py`'s `momentum` and `ma_cross` once monthly stepping, a cash leg and a vol cap are added, and Faber's turnover makes costs immaterial on KRX ETFs and tolerable on US ETFs through KIS. Moreira and Muir's volatility-managed portfolios raise the market factor's alpha by 4.9% a year and its Sharpe by 25% over 1926–2015, but Cederburg, O'Doherty, Wang and Yan show real-time versions of 103 strategies "generally earn lower certainty equivalent returns and Sharpe ratios" than the unmanaged portfolios, so the defensible form is a capped w_t = min(1, σ*/σ̂_t) with σ* fixed before the out-of-sample window and a direct Sharpe/certainty-equivalent comparison rather than a spanning alpha ([NBER w22208 pp. 3, 8, 13](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22208/w22208.pdf); [Cederburg et al. JFE 2020](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v138y2020i1p95-117.html)). Risk parity (the app's existing solver) rests on Asness, Frazzini and Pedersen's leverage-aversion argument that "safer assets must offer higher risk-adjusted returns", with the honest caveat that unlevered risk parity delivers lower return with lower risk and fails when stock–bond correlation turns positive as in 2022 ([AQR FAJ 2012](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/Journal-Article/Leverage-Aversion-and-Risk-Parity.pdf)). Bond-ETF carry follows Koijen, Moskowitz, Pedersen and Vrugt, whose bond carry is the yield slope plus roll-down (correlated 0.90 with the slope) and whose diversified carry portfolio earns a Sharpe of 1.1, implementable on TLT from FRED series the app already loads but blocked for KODEX 국고채 until a KRW curve source exists ([NBER w19325 pp. 4, 15–16](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w19325/w19325.pdf)). Gold's link to real yields (Erb and Harvey's −0.82 correlation between the TIPS real yield and the real gold price) is a hedge ratio and valuation z-score, not a signal ([NBER w18706](https://www.nber.org/papers/w18706)). Post-publication decay is the prior on every number: McLean and Pontiff find portfolio returns "26% lower out-of-sample and 58% lower post-publication" ([JF 2016](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1111/jofi.12365)).

### Index futures and options: build the surface first, then test the Korean short-volatility null

Contract facts are verified from broker disclosure documents: KOSPI200 futures and options use a **250,000 KRW multiplier** (the multiplier went from 100,000 to 500,000 on 15 June 2012 and down to 250,000 on 27 March 2017), ticks of 0.05 point (12,500 KRW) and 0.01 point (2,500 KRW) below a 10-point premium, expiry on the second Thursday, European cash settlement, and staged ±8/15/20% price limits; Thursday weeklies listed in September 2019 and Monday weeklies on 31 July 2023 with underlying code `AF` ([Meritz pp. 4, 11, 14, 16](https://home.imeritz.com/include/resource/bbs/contents/bascGrp/help13/20241111/yk24111102.pdf); [Sol Kim, JDQS 34(1) p. 6](https://www.emerald.com/jdqs/article-pdf/34/1/2/11102613/jdqs-09-2025-0060en.pdf); [Samsung Futures notice](https://www.ssfutures.com/ssf/customer/notice/CUS_01102.cmd?_PATH=Ad202_3811.shtm)). KIS's option board returns, per expiry, up to 100 calls and 100 puts with strike, bid, ask, last, open interest, delta, gamma, vega, theta, rho, `hts_ints_vltl` and `hts_thpr`, so the app can invert mids with its own Black-76 (`black76` and the Brent `_invert` in `core/ficc/swaps.py`) on the same-expiry futures price and reconcile against KIS's IV, check parity residuals against the bid-ask, and test vertical, butterfly and calendar inequalities daily ([display_board_callput](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/main/examples_llm/domestic_futureoption/display_board_callput/display_board_callput.py)). SVI with the Gatheral–Jacquier butterfly condition g(k) ≥ 0 is the right slice model for an equity index, with the existing Hagan SABR fitter kept as a cross-check at β = 1 ([Gatheral & Jacquier, QF 2014](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/14697688.2013.819986); [Hagan et al. 2002](https://www.deriscope.com/docs/Hagan_2002.pdf)). The Cboe strip σ² = (2/T)Σ(ΔK_i/K_i²)e^{RT}Q(K_i) − (1/T)[F/K_0 − 1]² with the two-consecutive-zero-bid truncation and 30-day interpolation reproduces VIX and, up to KRX's own "developed independently" adjustments, VKOSPI, which launched on 13 April 2009 on a fair-variance-swap method; the fitted SVI call price differentiated twice in strike gives the Breeden–Litzenberger density, and against the Quant Lab's GARCH-t terminal distribution the ratio f_Q/f_P is the empirical pricing kernel the user's own paper builds ([Cboe methodology pp. 7–9](https://cdn.cboe.com/api/global/us_indices/governance/Cboe_Volatility_Index_Mathematics_Methodology.pdf); [KB VKOSPI](https://kbthink.com/dictionary/view.html?dictId=KED-00014779); [Breeden & Litzenberger](https://ideas.repec.org/a/ucp/jnlbus/v51y1978i4p621-51.html)). None of this requires history: the board is a snapshot, KIS keeps no chain archive, and the surface history must be accumulated by a daily job at the close.

The futures basis is a monitor with a cost band, not a trade. The textbook forward is S/B(t,T) and equals the futures price only when rates are deterministic; the operational Korean fair value uses a 91-day CD rate and a dividend-amount index concentrated at the December record date, and Korea's empirical literature finds persistent underpricing tied to short-sale restrictions (Gay and Jung) and a measurable ex-dividend basis break: Ko reports the basis averaging 1.76 points before and 0.75 after the dividend, and Yuanta's 2010–2021 study finds the market basis contracting 0.85% at the open against a theoretical 1.18% ([Gay & Jung, JFM 1999](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/(SICI)1096-9934(199904)19:2%3C153::AID-FUT2%3E3.0.CO;2-S); [Ko 2022, KJFS](https://www.e-kjfs.org/journal/view.php?viewtype=pubreader&number=937); [edaily](https://edaily.co.kr/News/Read?mediaCodeNo=257&newsId=01226726632562784); [Mirae 2003 note](https://securities.miraeasset.com/bbs/download/2033615.pdf?attachmentId=2033615)). A caution the notes flag for the report writer: Bae, Chan and Cheung's bid-ask arbitrage paper is Hang Seng data, not KOSPI200 ([JFM 1998](https://ideas.repec.org/a/wly/jfutmk/v18y1998i7p743-763.html)). The implied funding rate r_impl = ((F + D)/S − 1)·365/τ compared with CD91 and KOFR is the desk-language output, and KOFR, the repo-based RFR published since November 2021 as CD rates are phased out by 2030, is the rate the club's SOFR-era conventions map to; its keyless daily access is unverified ([BOK KOFR](https://www.bok.or.kr/portal/main/contents.do?menuNo=201069)).

Short-volatility is where the Korean and US evidence diverge and the gate must hold a real null. Carr and Wu, Bollerslev–Tauchen–Zhou and Bakshi–Kapadia establish a large negative variance risk premium on US indices (delta-hedged long-option portfolios underperform zero), paid back in crashes: on 5 February 2018 VIX rose 115.6% and XIV lost 96.3% in a day, and short S&P 500 variance swaps lost 46% in the month to 20 March 2020 ([Carr & Wu](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/22/3/1311/1581057); [Bakshi & Kapadia](https://people.umass.edu/~nkapadia/docs/Bakshi_and_Kapadia_2003_RFS.pdf); [ETF.com](https://www.etf.com/sections/news/inverse-vix-etn-shuts-down); [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2020-04-03/the-booming-short-volatility-bet-that-lost-46-in-a-single-month)). **Yoon and Byun find volatility risk is not priced in KOSPI200 options**, that jump fears matter instead and that most option holders hold no underlying, and Sol Kim documents the post-2012 shift from retail to foreign-institution dominance that makes pre-2012 results stale ([Yoon & Byun, JFM 2009](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/fut.20386); [Sol Kim, JDQS](https://www.emerald.com/jdqs/article-pdf/34/1/2/11102613/jdqs-09-2025-0060en.pdf)). A daily delta-hedged short straddle needs three years of forward board snapshots, a 2천만원 deposit tier and ten days of open-interest history, and its pre-registered null is "mean after costs ≤ 0"; the app's structuring pages get the useful part now, a hedge-P&L simulator whose identity the user's own paper states as cost − e^{rT}V_0(σ_h) = ½∫e^{r(T−t)}Γ_t S_t²(σ_t² − σ_h²)dt, with the GARCH-t P-paths and the Black-76 Q-pricer already in the code. Option-implied predictors are weaker than they look: Nam, Lee, Kang and Choi find the KOSPI200 call–put IV spread forecasts returns largely because it encodes the spot–futures basis, and Xing, Zhang and Zhao's 10.9%-a-year smirk result is cross-sectional single-stock, not index timing ([KDA paper](https://www.kdajdqs.org/bbs/reference/867/download/1462); [JFQA 2010](https://www.ruf.rice.edu/~yxing/option-skew-FINAL.pdf)).

### Bonds and commodities: carry needs KOFR, curve forecasts need fifteen years, term structure needs two dated contracts

KTB futures are verified from 2025 disclosure documents: ₩100 million face, quoted per ₩100, tick 0.01 point (₩10,000) for 3-, 5- and 10-year and 0.02 point (₩20,000) for 30-year, March/June/September/December cycle with two listed months, last trade on the third Tuesday, cash settlement against a KOFIA-yield basket; the KIS commodity master identifies underlyings as `B03`/`B05`/`B10` and the exchange lists 3y (1999), 5y (2003), 10y (2008) and 30y (2024) contracts ([Yuanta pp. 14, 18](https://www.myasset.com/myasset/static/notice/250605_03.pdf); [KIS 종목마스터 header](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/koreainvestment/open-trading-api/main/stocks_info/%EC%A2%85%EB%AA%A9%EB%A7%88%EC%8A%A4%ED%84%B0%EC%A0%95%EB%B3%B4(%EC%83%81%ED%92%88%EC%84%A0%EB%AC%BC%EC%98%B5%EC%85%98).h); [기획재정부 국채시장](https://ktb.moef.go.kr/ntndbtFtrs.do)). The app already computes the carry object (`carry_roll`, `key_rate_dv01`, `futures_hedge_ratio`), so a KTB curve-carry strategy needs data plumbing (KIS bond quotes, KTB futures via a market code still to be probed, KOFR) rather than mathematics. Term-premium timing must be shipped as a diagnostic: Cochrane and Piazzesi's tent-shaped forward-rate factor reaches an in-sample R² of 0.44, but Thornton and Valente show forward-rate predictors "do not lead to higher out-of-sample Sharpe ratios or higher economic utility" than the expectations-hypothesis benchmark ([CP 2005 pp. 1–2](https://web.stanford.edu/~piazzesi/cp.pdf); [Thornton & Valente, RFS 2012](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/25/10/3141/1573606)). Diebold and Li's dynamic Nelson–Siegel with λ fixed at 0.0609 and per-factor AR(1) forecasts beats the random walk decisively only at 12 months (RMSE 0.74–0.98 versus 1.02–1.24 percentage points) and is "only very slightly better" at one month; the app's `fit_nelson_siegel` needs λ fixed and at least 15 years of FRED `DGS*` rather than the current 504-session panel ([NBER w10048 pp. 12, 16, 29–31](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w10048/w10048.pdf)). The 2-5-10 butterfly with PCA-neutral weights is a stationarity test on the fly level with a pre-registered half-life bound, and MOP's bond futures TSMOM is implementable today on `ZT=F`, `ZF=F`, `ZN=F` and `ZB=F` with the caveat that Yahoo's continuous-contract construction is undocumented.

Commodity models turn on one data fact: **the basis needs two dated contracts, and Yahoo's `=F` symbols give one spliced series whose roll treatment is unverified**. Gorton, Hayashi and Rouwenhorst find the high-minus-low basis portfolio earns 10.23% a year (t = 3.73) and 12-month futures momentum 13.36% (t = 4.93) over 31 commodities 1969–2006; Szymanowska et al. split premia into spot (5–14% on sorts) and term (1–3%) components; Miffre and Rallis show momentum "buy[s] backwardated contracts and sell[s] contangoed contracts" ([GHR NBER 13249 pp. 2, 21, 23](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w13249/w13249.pdf); [Szymanowska et al.](https://www.tias.edu/docs/default-source/Kennisartikelen/an-anatomy-of-commodity-futures-risk-premia-szymanowska-and-de-roon-and-nijman-and-van-den-goorbergh-(2013).pdf?sfvrsn=6); [Miffre & Rallis](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S037842660700026X)). KIS `daily-ccnl` on the two nearest dated contracts, 40 rows per call, is therefore required for any term-structure signal, and it lets the app build its own continuous series with a known adjustment. The Treasury cash–futures basis trade, which grew to about half of hedge funds' Treasury positions and a quarter of dealer repo lending before March 2020, is measurable only as a proxy (synthetic CTD from the FRED curve, SOFR as the funding bound) because the actual CTD price and term repo are not in any keyless feed; it is a Macro-page funding-stress read, not a trade ([OFR WP 21-01](https://www.financialresearch.gov/working-papers/2021/04/01/hedge-funds-and-the-treasury-cash-futures-disconnect/); [CFTC MRAC pp. 2–5](https://www.cftc.gov/media/11671/mrac121024_TreasuryCashFuturesBasisTrade/download)). One inconsistency in the app surfaces here and is worth fixing: `ratesim` calibrates Vasicek and CIR by OLS on the historical short rate (a P-estimate) and reprices with the same parameters as if they were Q, i.e. with a zero market price of risk; the reconciliation is to keep κ and σ from P, identify λ by forcing the t = 0 model curve onto the bootstrapped curve, and report the P-distribution of Q-prices, which is Meucci's VaR use of Monte Carlo ([Meucci §3.1–3.3](file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트%20학습%20자료/P%20versus%20Q%20_%20Differences%20and%20Commonalities%20between%20the%20Two%20Areas%20of%20Quantitative%20Finance.pdf)).

### Overseas and KRX single names: factor monitors and long-only tilts survive, pairs mostly do not

Ken French's library serves keyless daily zips for US 3-factor (from 1926-07-01), 5-factor, momentum, short- and long-term reversal, and for Developed-ex-US, Japan and Asia-Pacific-ex-Japan (daily from 1990-07-01), but **Korea appears only in the monthly Emerging Markets files**, so Korean factor regressions need home-built SMB/HML/MOM from pykrx market caps and PBR ([Data Library](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/data_library.html); [Emerging detail](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/f-f_5emerging.html)). The rolling-regression monitor with Newey–West standard errors is a measurement tool judged against Harvey, Liu and Zhu's t > 3.0 hurdle, and Hou, Xue and Zhang's finding that 65% of 452 anomalies fail even t = 1.96 once microcaps are neutralised (82% at 2.78) is the prior for every cross-sectional claim ([RFS 2016](https://academic.oup.com/rfs/article-abstract/29/1/5/1843824); [Hou–Xue–Zhang](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v33y2020i5p2019-2133..html)). Momentum's international map argues for testing rather than assuming Korea: Fama and French find "except for Japan, there is return momentum everywhere", Chui, Titman and Wei find momentum higher in individualistic countries, Daniel and Moskowitz document crashes "in panic states, following market declines and when market volatility is high", and Jacobs and Müller find the US "is the only country with a reliable post-publication decline" among 39 markets, which together imply Korean anomalies, if present, persist longer but are harder to harvest at 35 bp per side including tax ([FF 2012](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v105y2012i3p457-472.html); [Chui–Titman–Wei](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1111/j.1540-6261.2009.01532.x?fields=title,authors,year,abstract); [Daniel–Moskowitz](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v122y2016i2p221-247.html); [Jacobs–Müller](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v135y2020i1p213-230.html)).

Pairs trading is the family whose evidence and Korean frictions collide. Gatev, Goetzmann and Rouwenhorst's distance rule earned "up to 11%" a year 1962–2002, but Do and Faff show the top-20-pair return falling from 0.86% a month (1962–88) to 0.37% (1989–2002) to 0.24% (2003–09), and Avellaneda and Lee's PCA stat-arb Sharpe fell from 1.44 to 0.9 ([GGR](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v19y2006i3p797-827.html); [Do & Faff](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/ufajxx/v66y2010i4p83-95.html); [Avellaneda & Lee](https://ideas.repec.org/a/taf/quantf/v10y2010i7p761-782.html)). At a 0.74% round trip per KRX pair, 0.24% a month cannot survive unless half-lives are weeks and entries wide, and the short leg needs 개인대주, which after the 31 March 2025 resumption runs on 90-day rolling terms up to one year at 105% collateral and only in names on KIS's `lendable-by-company` list ([Namu wiki 공매도](https://namu.wiki/w/공매도)). The econometric backbone, ADF, Engle–Granger two-step, Johansen and the VECM, sits in the user's 계량2 textbook with verified page offsets, and the one Korean pair with no sell tax, KODEX 200 versus TIGER 200, is a teaching pair for the half-life machinery rather than a source of return. Korean flow data is the genuinely local edge to test: Choe, Kho and Stulz find "strong evidence of positive feedback trading and herding by foreign investors" and a 21 bp / 16 bp trading disadvantage for foreign money managers "because prices move more against foreign investors than against domestic investors before trades", Kim and Wei find non-resident institutions "always positive feedback traders", but none of this establishes next-day stock-level predictability, so both the continuation and the reversal hypothesis are registered and the panel decides ([NBER w6661](https://www.nber.org/papers/w6661); [RFS 2005](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/rfinst/v18y2005i3p795-829.html); [Kim & Wei](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/inecon/v56y2002i1p77-96.html)). Machine-learning return prediction after Gu, Kelly and Xiu is feasible only as a linear-regularised replication (no scikit-learn is installed), and the user's own `tut03_cv.R` grouped-by-region random folds guard entity leakage but not temporal leakage, so the folds must become contiguous blocks with purging and an embargo ([NBER w25398](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25398)). Same-issuer spreads round out the list: Gagnon and Karolyi's ADR parity deviations average "an economically small 4.9 basis points", below one KRX round trip, so the ADR premium is a signal for which venue to buy in, and the 우선주 discount is a long-only allocation tilt whose cross-section KIS serves directly through `prefer-disparate-ratio` ([Gagnon & Karolyi](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jfinec/v97y2010i1p53-80.html)).

The crypto models with peer-reviewed backing are few and short-horizon. Liu and Tsyvinski find Bitcoin has "no exposure to most common stock market and macroeconomic factors" over 2011–2018, a strong time-series momentum where a one-standard-deviation rise in this week's return predicts +3.16% next week and +0.33% the next day, and attention effects; Liu, Tsyvinski and Wu build a three-factor model (market, size, 1–4-week momentum) on every CoinMarketCap coin above $1 million, 109 in 2014 to 1,583 in 2018, in which 3-week momentum earns 4.1% a week and 8-week-and-longer sorts are insignificant ([NBER w24877](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w24877/w24877.pdf); [NBER w25882](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w25882/w25882.pdf)). ## One gate decides admission: walk-forward Sharpe interval, deflated Sharpe ≥ 0.95, PBO ≤ 0.05, PIT-calibrated fans

The user's own folder already contains every principle the gate needs, which is why the protocol cites those texts first. CS229 §9.3 states that selecting the hypothesis with the smallest training error "does not work" and prescribes hold-out and k-fold validation (pp. 139–142); Tibshirani's Lecture 6 §5.2 defines time-series cross-validation as retraining at each origin and warns that selecting the ARIMA order once on all data is the "wrong way" because "we would have used the future", adding that "the entire procedure must be rerun each time predictions are to be validated" (pp. 21–22); Lecture 8 §3 defines empirical coverage "in a time series CV sense", the online quantile-tracking repair, and PIT calibration, where a U-shaped histogram means the fan is too narrow and an inverted U too wide (pp. 9–11); STAT248 §21–22 explains why in-sample log-likelihood and AIC are only asymptotic proxies for out-of-sample fit and states the noise-versus-bias trade-off of splitting a sample (pp. 96–106); the 계량2 textbook gives Mincer–Zarnowitz, RMSE and Theil's U (§2.4.3, pp. 85–88) but cites Diebold–Mariano only in a reference list; the user's roadmap names purged and embargoed cross-validation, the Harvey–Liu–Zhu t ≈ 3 hurdle and the deflated Sharpe ratio as "the two ideas an interviewer will use to check whether your backtest results are believable" (Two Doors p. 8), and the user's own paper warns that "P-estimates of tails need decades of data" (RN→FH §3.4 p. 9) ([CS229](<file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트 학습 자료/CS229(Machine Learning, Stanford)/main_notes.pdf>); [Lecture006](<file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트 학습 자료/Introduction to Time Series (UC Berkeley)/Lecture006.pdf>); [Lecture008](<file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트 학습 자료/Introduction to Time Series (UC Berkeley)/Lecture008.pdf>); [STAT248](<file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트 학습 자료/STAT248(UC Berkeley)/Applied Time Series - Full Lecture Notes.pdf>); [Two Doors](<file:///C:/Users/whydi/iCloudDrive/퀀트 학습 자료/Quant_Roadmap_Two_Doors_GEON_KOH_1.pdf>)). The literature supplies the statistics. Bailey and López de Prado's deflated Sharpe ratio corrects for the number of trials through the expected maximum of N null Sharpe ratios, SR₀ = σ_SR[(1−γ)Φ⁻¹(1−1/N) + γΦ⁻¹(1−1/(Ne))] with γ = 0.5772, and DSR = Φ[(ŜR − SR₀)√(T−1)/√(1 − γ̂₃ŜR + ((γ̂₄−1)/4)ŜR²)], where the denominator is confirmed only through secondary sources and must be checked against equation (2) of the paper before release; their worked example concludes that an annualised Sharpe of 2.5 after 100 trials over 1,250 days is not significant ([deflated-sharpe.pdf pp. 7–10, 15](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf)). Bailey, Borwein, López de Prado and Zhu define the probability of backtest overfitting as the probability that the in-sample optimum ranks below the out-of-sample median across all C(S, S/2) combinatorially symmetric splits of the trial P&L matrix, and state that "a customary approach would be to reject models for which PBO is estimated to be greater than 0.05" ([backtest-prob.pdf pp. 10–14](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)). Lo's iid standard error √((1 + ŜR²/2)/T) gives the sample-size arithmetic that a one-sided 90% interval excludes zero only if T > 252·(1.645/SR_ann)² sessions, so **a strategy with an annualised Sharpe of 0.5 needs about 2,730 sessions (10.8 years) and a two-year record cannot certify anything below SR ≈ 1.2**; Ledoit and Wolf's studentised block bootstrap handles heavy tails and dependence ([Two Sigma TR 2018-001](https://www.twosigma.com/wp-content/uploads/sharpe-tr-1.pdf); [Ledoit & Wolf](https://ideas.repec.org/p/zur/iewwpx/320.html)). For distributional models Kupiec's unconditional-coverage likelihood ratio has only 65% power in one year to catch a 3%-for-1% VaR, rising to about 90% in two; Christoffersen's Markov test adds independence and conditional coverage; Diebold, Gunther and Tay require the PIT sequence to be iid U(0,1); the Basel traffic light colours 0–4, 5–9 and ≥10 exceptions of 99% VaR over 250 days green, yellow and red; and Gneiting and Raftery's log score and CRPS = E|X − x| − ½E|X − X′| are the strictly proper scores for ranking fans ([Campbell, FEDS 2005-21 pp. 5–13](https://www.federalreserve.gov/pubs/feds/2005/200521/200521pap.pdf); [DGT 1998](https://archive.nyu.edu/bitstream/2451/14779/1/SOR-98-6.pdf); [Gneiting & Raftery](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf)). The binomial arithmetic sets the floors: to detect an 8% exceedance rate against a 5% target at 80% power needs about 340–480 one-day origins, and 2% against 1% needs about 1,000, so the gate requires at least 500 one-day origins for VaR95 tests and 1,000 for VaR99.

## Conclusion

The research reverses the premise that expanding QUANTGEN means new brokers or new credentials. The app key it already has is the widest read-only key a Korean retail quant can hold, and the binding constraints are elsewhere: history that KIS does not archive (option chains, futures investor flows, a KRW benchmark curve, expired contracts), rates the app cannot yet fetch keylessly (daily CD91 and KOFR), and a set of two probe calls that will tell in an afternoon whether derivatives and overseas quotes answer to a stock-account key. The same pattern holds for crypto, where the decision is not which exchange to sign up with but to make the app indifferent to exchange accounts altogether, since the price, funding, open-interest and on-chain data are all public and the only credential worth holding is a one-year, read-only Upbit key whose real value is a cost-basis record before 1 January 2027. What emerges is a division of labour the FICC club will recognise: KIS and keyless feeds supply the P-side data, the user's own Shreve, Meucci and Radon–Nikodym material supply the Q-side pricing, and the gate turns the two into one artefact per model, a card that says PASS, REVISE, REJECT or, most often for Korean histories, INSUFFICIENT DATA.

The candidates most likely to survive that gate are the ones with no alpha claim: the leveraged-ETF drag forecast, the Black-76 board reconciliation, the futures basis monitor, the hedge-P&L simulator, the Diebold–Li forecast and the out-of-sample scoring of the GARCH-t fan on Bitcoin, each of which produces a number the desk speaks in (bp of notional, vol points, bp of richness) and each of which can fail loudly. The trend, carry and momentum strategies come next because their evidence is deepest and their turnover lowest, and every idea whose edge lives inside one Korean round trip, ETF premium reversal on the flagships, same-index pairs, weekly reversal, KOSPI200 short volatility, is registered to be shelved when the data says so. That is the report's most useful finding for the club application: a written prior that a strategy will fail, tested and recorded, is worth more in this project than a strategy that appears to work.
