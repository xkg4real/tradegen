Macro Neural Link

Six simulated professors and seven simulated finance executives argued over how macroeconomics should enter QUANTGEN. Every claim was then tested on 1926–2026 data. Macro earns a place as risk sizing, data hygiene, hedge checks and event handling. Almost every rule that used macro to time direction failed.

139resolved claims, after 13 position papers and 7 cross-examinations
27core links both panels backed, written into the integration spec
11prohibitions: ideas that failed the burden of proof (harm, null or inconclusive), listed as do-not-build
22backlog items still contested, one-sided or untested

Keep

Momentum sized by its own risk

Own-volatility scaling lifts US momentum's Sharpe from 0.40 to 0.71 under the spec's 1.0× cap, and to 0.80 with a floored bear-market gate, which fails the multiple-testing step and stays in the specification book. Max drawdown falls from −63% to −28% (−21% with the gate).

Keep as a labelled control

Korea-local volatility control

A 20-day KOSPI vol target cut the 22 Jun–30 Jul 2026 crash from −38.6% to −12.2%. No US indicator flagged it. It also gave up half of the H1 rally. On the long KOSPI history (1997–2026) it is an indicative pass at QUANTGEN's ETF cost, but a fragile one: July 2026 supplies 49% of its benefit against a 50% limit, and 2016–25 alone is a reject. Under spec v2.0 its formal run is expected to return INSUFFICIENT DATA once the trials already run on Korean data are counted (selection factor 1.26–2.87 against a 1.282 limit), so the card is not expected to build.

Drop

Macro as a direction signal

Curve, real-rate, credit, oil and VIX-term-structure gates, the prior four-state table and every 1.5× multiplier lost value. The prior Korea gate's +0.31 Sharpe came from look-ahead.

    2 · The macro tape, Jan 2024 → Sep 2026

    Cutting into a soft labor market, then hiking into an oil shock

    The Fed cut 175 bp between Sep 2024 and Dec 2025, then hiked on 16 Sep 2026 as inflation, an energy shock and record term premium pushed the 10-year to 5.29%. Korea had its own cycle: a memory-chip boom took KOSPI to 9,114 before a 38.6% crash.

      4 · What survived the data

      Volatility rules survived. Macro direction rules did not.

      The prior design's overlay rules were back-tested on daily factor returns from 1990 with information lagged one day, then bootstrapped. Survivors were re-run under the spec's caps and at QUANTGEN's own costs.

      Korea and crypto rules. The prior Korea gate (semiconductor trend × won) showed +0.31 Sharpe only because it used US data that arrive after the KOSPI close. Lagged correctly it adds +0.05, and the semiconductor leg alone lost 0.22 in 2024–26. Scaling bitcoin by US net liquidity added −0.01 overall and −0.20 in 2023–26; bitcoin fell 53% while net liquidity rose.

      5 · The debate

      What the panels disagreed on, and what settled it

      Each panelist cross-examined colleagues in the same panel, re-opened sources and re-ran numbers. Several positions were withdrawn by their own authors.

      QuestionProfessorsExecutivesWhat settled it
      How much does momentum crash protection add?P3 cited Daniel–Moskowitz as 1.43 vs 0.71Use it, but simplyThe chair re-opened the published JFE 2016 paper: 0.682 static, 1.041 with constant vol, 1.194 dynamic. Most of the gain is plain vol scaling.
      Does macro add anything at daily horizons?P6: no, use the market's own states. P1: macro decides scope (hedges, inflation regime).Only as risk inputsBoth: no macro gate on daily equity exposure, but macro decides whether bond and dollar hedges count.

      6 · Into QUANTGEN

      What the integration spec builds, runs forward, and forbids

      QUANTGEN is a read-only research desk, so every rule becomes a model card that passes through its nine-step gate. Nothing here places an order.

      Build now

      Core links

      • Point-in-time dataUTC and publication timestamps, vintages, US data usable only from the next KRX session, missing months fail closed.
      • Bounded overlaysEvery overlay multiplier in [0.5, 1.0] and every vol-target weight in [0, 1]; nothing above 1.0; zero exposure only from the risk layer or the engine's HALTED state.
      • Momentum risk stateOwn-vol scaling (cap 1.0); the bear gate (floor 0.5) runs in the specification book only, because it fails the multiple-testing step (0.77).
      • Per-market vol targetKorea 20-day, cap 1.0, Samsung + SK hynix caps. SMA20 as the displayed baseline: it passes the calibration backtest but sits outside the forecast-accuracy confidence set, so spec v2 picks the sizing estimator with a three-stage gate (R-30: calibration, a screen on the 90% QLIKE model confidence set, then the choice at the declared book); long-window GARCH-t and GJR-t already pass the same backtest.
      • Hedge-validity stateNo bond-hedge credit at core PCE ≥3% or positive 63-day stock–bond correlation.
      • Events and contextUS nights as KOSPI open-gap inputs; oil to the event calendar and Monte Carlo; Sahm, curve, Taylor and term premium logged, never acting.

      Forward-sim and next tests

      Running on paper, or waiting for data

      • USD/KRW leg20-day rise above 3% → Korea gross 0.75×. Rejected on the 1997–2026 history at every timing (not de-risked on the worst days); it keeps running on paper as a labelled refusal. A timestamped won history is no longer needed to decide it.
      • US rates leg10-year +40 bp in 20 days → US duration-sensitive gross 0.5×. No live feed is needed: Treasury posts each day's par yields by about 6:00 pm ET, so a decision at the next close can use them with a logged posting time (via H.15, which posts at 16:15 ET the next business day, only two business days later). Rejected at every timing and cost, gross included; it keeps running on paper as a labelled refusal.
      • Korea leverage state (backlog)Margin debt, leveraged-ETF assets and investor flows via KIS. Top backlog priority.
      • Bear-gate holdouts (done)The pre-registered out-of-region tests passed in 1 of 3 regions, so the Daniel–Moskowitz gate stays in the specification book.

      Do not build

      Prohibited by the evidence

      • Prior four-state tableand every multiplier above 1.0×.
      • VIX-term-structure momentum switchIt predicts volatility, not direction.
      • Curve, real-rate, credit and oil gateson factor weights or market gross.
      • Semiconductor × won Korea gateLook-ahead artefact.
      • Net-liquidity crypto rule, Street forecasts and surveys as signals
      • Smoothed regime probabilities, macro ML "complexity" timing, pre-cutoff LLM backtests, swing blackouts
